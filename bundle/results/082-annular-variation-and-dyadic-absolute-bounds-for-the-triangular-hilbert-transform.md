# 082. Annular variation and dyadic absolute bounds for the triangular Hilbert transform

> Discipline: [Real and complex analysis](../disciplines/real-and-complex-analysis.md)
> Manuscripts: **3**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/082.md)

## Description

Proves maximal and annular <i>r</i>-variation bounds, for every <i>r</i> &gt; 2, from complex $`L^3(\mathbb R^2)\times L^3(\mathbb R^2)`$ to $`L^{3/2}(\mathbb R^2)`$. The maximal estimate controls both hard truncation endpoints and gives almost-everywhere and norm convergence. Pairing with a third input settles the triangular Hilbert transform estimate at the symmetric $`L^3\times L^3\times L^3`$ point.
