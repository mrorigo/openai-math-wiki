# 314. Cyclic length and chromatic fixed-point loss

> Discipline: [Topology](../disciplines/topology.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Determines the optimal chromatic loss from geometric <i>H</i>-fixed points to geometric <i>G</i>-fixed points for every subgroup <i>H</i> of a finite <i>p</i>-group <i>G</i>. At every nonnegative height, the loss equals the shortest subnormal-chain length from <i>H</i> to <i>G</i> with cyclic quotients. Each quotient counts once regardless of order, and finite spectra witness sharpness.
