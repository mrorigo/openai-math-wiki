# 125. The metric <i>k</i>-median approximation threshold and recovery

> Discipline: [Theoretical computer science](../disciplines/theoretical-computer-science.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/125.md)

## Description

Gives a deterministic polynomial-time $`(1+2/e+\varepsilon)`$-approximation for finite rational metric <i>k</i>-median with specified candidate facilities, for every fixed <i>ε</i> &gt; 0. Assuming $`P\ne NP`$, the optimal infimum approximation factor is $`1+2/e`$.
