# 039. Nagata’s conjecture and maximal Seshadri constants

> Discipline: [Algebraic and complex geometry](../disciplines/algebraic-and-complex-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **4**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/039.md)

## Description

Proves Nagata's strict inequality $`\sum_i m_i\lt d\sqrt r`$ for every nonzero effective plane curve of degree <i>d</i> through <i>r</i> ≥ 10 very general complex points, with arbitrary multiplicities <i>m</i><sub><i>i</i></sub>. It also proves maximal multipoint Seshadri constants $`(L^n/r)^{1/n}`$ for every smooth polarized projective variety of dimension <i>n</i> ≥ 2 and all sufficiently large <i>r</i>: at very general points over ℂ, and at the geometric generic tuple over any algebraically closed field of positive characteristic.
