# 194. Lech’s multiplicity conjecture

> Discipline: [Algebra](../disciplines/algebra.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/194.md)

## Description

Proves $`e(R)\le e(S)`$ for every flat local homomorphism of nonzero Noetherian local rings, where <i>e</i> is Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity. This resolves Lech's conjecture in every dimension and characteristic.
