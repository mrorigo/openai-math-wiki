# 215. Canonical $`O(3)`$ continuum limit and exact $`O(4)`$ mass asymptotics

> Discipline: [Probability and statistical mechanics](../disciplines/probability-and-statistical-mechanics.md)
> Manuscripts: **5**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/215.md)

## Description

Constructs the canonical continuum limit of the two-dimensional nearest-neighbor $`O(3)`$ model: a non-Gaussian local relativistic theory with a unique vacuum and a positive mass gap. For the square-lattice $`O(4)`$ model, determines the exact leading asymptotic of the full transfer gap, $`m_{\mathrm{lat}}(\beta)\sim32e^{\pi/4-1/2}\sqrt\beta\,e^{-\pi\beta}`$. The family also proves exponential spin-correlation decay for two-dimensional nearest-neighbor $`O(n)`$ models with <i>n</i> ≥ 3 at every positive temperature.
