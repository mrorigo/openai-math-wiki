# 026. Positive lower density of large prime gaps

> Discipline: [Number theory](../disciplines/number-theory.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/026.md)

## Description

For every fixed <i>C</i> &gt; 0, a positive proportion of consecutive prime gaps exceed $`C\log p_n`$, throughout every sufficiently large initial segment of the primes. The proportion may depend on <i>C</i>. Consequently, the indices where $`p_n/n`$ increases have positive lower density, answering Erdős and Prachar.
