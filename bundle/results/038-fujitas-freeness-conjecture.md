# 038. Fujita’s freeness conjecture

> Discipline: [Algebraic and complex geometry](../disciplines/algebraic-and-complex-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Proves Fujita's freeness conjecture at its sharp bound in every dimension: for a smooth projective complex variety <i>X</i> of dimension <i>n</i> and an ample line bundle <i>L</i>, the adjoint $`K_X+mL`$ is globally generated for every integer $`m\ge n+1`$.
