# 230. Exact Hausdorff gauges for SLE

> Discipline: [Probability and statistical mechanics](../disciplines/probability-and-statistical-mechanics.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/230.md)

## Description

Resolves Schramm’s Hausdorff-measure question for chordal SLE<sub><i>κ</i></sub>, $`0\lt \kappa\lt 8`$. The explicit gauge $`r^d(\log\log(1/r))^{(2-d)/2}`$, $`d=1+\kappa/8`$, gives almost surely positive finite measure to every trace segment $`\gamma([s,t])`$ with $`0\lt s\lt t\lt \infty`$, and finite expected measure to the trace in every bounded disk.
