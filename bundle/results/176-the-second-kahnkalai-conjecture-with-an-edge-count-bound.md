# 176. The second Kahn–Kalai conjecture with an edge-count bound

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/176.md)

## Description

Proves the second Kahn–Kalai conjecture: for every finite simple graph <i>H</i> with <i>h</i> ≥ 1 edges and at most <i>n</i> vertices, its appearance threshold in $`G(n,p)`$ is at most $`C p_{\mathrm E}(n,H)(1+\log_2 h)`$, with universal <i>C</i>. Here $`p_{\mathrm E}`$ is the least density at which every subgraph of <i>H</i> has expected copy count at least 1/2.
