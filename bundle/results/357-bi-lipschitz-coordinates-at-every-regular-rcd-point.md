# 357. Bi-Lipschitz coordinates at every regular RCD point

> Discipline: [Differential geometry](../disciplines/differential-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Proves that every regular point of a noncollapsed $`\mathop{\mathrm{RCD}}\nolimits (K,n)`$ space, for <i>K</i> ∈ ℝ and integer <i>n</i> ≥ 2, has an open neighborhood bi-Lipschitz to an open subset of ℝ<sup><i>n</i></sup>. Regularity requires all pointed tangents to be Euclidean, the reference measure is exactly $`\mathcal H^n`$, and the chart compares ambient distances with a point-dependent finite constant.
