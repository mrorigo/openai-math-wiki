# 107. Matrix multiplication with exponent at most 9/4

> Discipline: [Theoretical computer science](../disciplines/theoretical-computer-science.md)
> Manuscripts: **3**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/107.md)

## Description

Proves $`\omega\le9/4`$ over ℂ, giving $`O_\varepsilon(n^{9/4+\varepsilon})`$ arithmetic operations for square matrix multiplication. In characteristic zero, some inner dimension <i>n</i><sup><i>a</i></sup> with <i>a</i> &gt; 0.465 permits $`n^{2+o(1)}`$ rectangular multiplication. Further square bounds give <i>ω</i> &lt; 2.258 outside finitely many positive characteristics and <i>ω</i> &lt; 2.371054886006746 over every fixed field.
