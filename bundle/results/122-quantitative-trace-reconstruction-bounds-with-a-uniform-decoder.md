# 122. Quantitative trace-reconstruction bounds with a uniform decoder

> Discipline: [Theoretical computer science](../disciplines/theoretical-computer-science.md)
> Manuscripts: **3**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/122.md)

## Description

At every fixed deletion probability in $`(0,1)`$, reconstructing an arbitrary length-<i>n</i> binary string requires $`n^{\Omega(\log\log n)}`$ independent traces, ruling out polynomial-sample reconstruction. A uniform decoder achieves quasipolynomial sample and running-time bounds for known fixed rational retention probabilities. When the deletion probability is at most $`n^{-\varepsilon}`$ for fixed <i>ε</i> &gt; 0, both bounds become polynomial in the input and parameter encoding.
