# 223. Random-cluster interfaces: critical, disordered, thermal, and natural-time scaling

> Discipline: [Probability and statistical mechanics](../disciplines/probability-and-statistical-mechanics.md)
> Manuscripts: **6**

## Description

Proves chordal SLE<sub><i>κ</i></sub> limits for critical square-lattice random-cluster interfaces for $`0\lt q\le4`$, with $`\kappa=4\pi/\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)`$: bounded Jordan domains are allowed for <i>q</i> ≥ 1, and smooth Jordan domains for <i>q</i> &lt; 1, under the stated marked-boundary approximations. For $`1\le q\le4`$, complete nested plane loops converge to CLE<sub><i>κ</i></sub>.
