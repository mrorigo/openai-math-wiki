# 349. The Solomon–Yau least-volume conjecture

> Discipline: [Differential geometry](../disciplines/differential-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Proves the Solomon–Yau least-volume conjecture for minimal hypersurfaces of round spheres. For every <i>m</i> ≥ 2, a closed connected minimal immersion into the unit sphere $`S^{m+1}`$ with non-totally-geodesic image has volume at least that of the smallest minimal Clifford product, counting covering multiplicity.
