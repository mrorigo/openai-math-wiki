# 374. Sharp one-third stability of Brenier maps

> Discipline: [Partial differential equations](../disciplines/partial-differential-equations.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/374.md)

## Description

For uniform source measure <i>ρ</i> on a compact convex body with interior in dimension at least two, quadratic optimal transport maps satisfy $`\|T_\mu-T_\nu\|_{L^2(\rho)}\le C W_2(\mu,\nu)^{1/3}`$ uniformly over targets in a fixed compact set. The exponent is sharp, even for three-atom targets, disproving Letrouit's conjectured square-root bound.
