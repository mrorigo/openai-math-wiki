# 035. Log-canonical threefold abundance in numerical dimension one

> Discipline: [Algebraic and complex geometry](../disciplines/algebraic-and-complex-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

## Description

Proves log abundance for projective log canonical threefold pairs over algebraically closed fields of characteristic <i>p</i> &gt; 3 when the effective boundary is rational and the ℚ-Cartier adjoint is nef of numerical dimension one. The adjoint is semiample, without requiring the original variety to be terminal or ℚ-factorial.
