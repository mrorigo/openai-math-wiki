# 262. Sharp finite-matrix Lieb–Thirring inequalities and all equality cases

> Discipline: [Mathematical physics](../disciplines/mathematical-physics.md)
> Manuscripts: **3**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/262.md)

## Description

Proves the sharp one-dimensional Lieb–Thirring inequality for $`1/2\lt \gamma\lt 3/2`$ and arbitrary finite-matrix potentials <i>W</i> ≥ 0 with $`\int\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\nolimits (W^{\gamma+1/2})\lt \infty`$: the optimal constant is the scalar one-bound-state value, independent of matrix size. All equality cases are direct sums, in one constant unitary basis, of scalar sech<sup>2</sup> solitons with independent scales and centers, and zero channels.
