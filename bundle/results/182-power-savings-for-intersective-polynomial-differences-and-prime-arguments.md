# 182. Power savings for intersective polynomial differences and prime arguments

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **3**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/182.md)

## Description

For every fixed intersective integer polynomial <i>h</i> of degree <i>k</i> ≥ 2 with positive leading coefficient, proves that a subset of $`\{1,\ldots,N\}`$ avoiding nonzero values $`h(1),h(2),\ldots`$ as differences has size $`O_h(N^{1-c_k})`$, with $`c_k\gt 0`$ depending only on degree. Here intersective means having a root modulo every modulus. For prime arguments, a power saving also holds when <i>h</i> has a unit root modulo every modulus, with exponent allowed to depend on <i>h</i>.
