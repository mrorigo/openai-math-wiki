# 096. The Gaussian propeller conjecture in every dimension

> Discipline: [Convex and metric geometry](../disciplines/convex-and-metric-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/096.md)

## Description

Proves that the sum of squared Gaussian first moments of any finite measurable partition is at most $`9/(8\pi)`$. In dimension at least two, three planar sectors of angle $`2\pi/3`$, extended orthogonally, attain the bound. Combined with the separate Unique Games theorem, this proves NP-hardness of improving the loss factor $`(8\pi/9)(1-1/k)`$ for identity-target kernel clustering with fixed <i>k</i> ≥ 3 on rational centered positive semidefinite inputs.
