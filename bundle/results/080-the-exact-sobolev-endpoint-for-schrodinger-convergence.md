# 080. The exact Sobolev endpoint for Schrödinger convergence

> Discipline: [Real and complex analysis](../disciplines/real-and-complex-analysis.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

## Description

Proves almost-everywhere convergence $`e^{it\Delta}f\to f`$ as $`t\downarrow0`$ for every $`f\in H^{n/(2(n+1))}(\mathbb R^n)`$ and every dimension <i>n</i> ≥ 2. This attains the sharp Sobolev equality case of Carleson's Schrödinger convergence problem, including the planar endpoint <i>H</i><sup>1/3</sup>.
