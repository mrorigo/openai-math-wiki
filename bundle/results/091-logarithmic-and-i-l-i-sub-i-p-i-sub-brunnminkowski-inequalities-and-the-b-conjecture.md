# 091. Logarithmic and <i>L</i><sub><i>p</i></sub> Brunn–Minkowski inequalities and the B-conjecture

> Discipline: [Convex and metric geometry](../disciplines/convex-and-metric-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/091.md)

## Description

Proves the logarithmic Brunn–Minkowski inequality for origin-symmetric convex bodies in every dimension, and the scalar-dilation B-conjecture for all even log-concave Radon measures. For Lebesgue volume it also proves the additive <i>L</i><sub><i>p</i></sub> Brunn–Minkowski inequality for full-dimensional origin-symmetric convex bodies throughout $`0\lt p\lt 1`$.
