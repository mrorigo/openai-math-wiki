# 156. Borsuk's conjecture fails in dimension nine

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/156.md)

## Description

Constructs a compact subset of ℝ<sup>9</sup> that cannot be covered by ten sets of strictly smaller diameter, disproving Borsuk's covering assertion already in dimension nine. The example consists of rank-one orthogonal projectors onto lines in ℝ<sup>4</sup>, with the Frobenius metric.
