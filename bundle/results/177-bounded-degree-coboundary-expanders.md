# 177. Bounded-degree coboundary expanders

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/177.md)

## Description

Constructs arbitrarily large finite <i>d</i>-dimensional simplicial complexes, for every <i>d</i> ≥ 3, with uniformly bounded vertex degrees and uniform 𝔽<sub>2</sub> coboundary expansion in every degree below <i>d</i>. Together with the known graph and two-dimensional cases, this establishes the existence of such expanders in every positive dimension.
