# 189. Cycle–clique Ramsey numbers

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/189.md)

## Description

Proves the Erdős–Faudree–Rousseau–Schelp conjecture: $`R(C_m,K_n)=(m-1)(n-1)+1`$ for every $`m\ge n\ge3`$, except $`R(C_3,K_3)=6`$. This is the exact threshold forcing a red <i>m</i>-cycle or a blue <i>n</i>-clique in every red–blue coloring of a complete graph.
