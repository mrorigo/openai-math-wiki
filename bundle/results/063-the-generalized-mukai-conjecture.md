# 063. The generalized Mukai conjecture

> Discipline: [Algebraic and complex geometry](../disciplines/algebraic-and-complex-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Proves the generalized Mukai conjecture: every positive-dimensional smooth complex projective Fano manifold of dimension <i>n</i>, Picard number <i>ρ</i> and pseudoindex <i>ι</i> satisfies $`\rho(\iota-1)\le n`$, with equality exactly for $`(\mathbb P^{\iota-1})^\rho`$. Here the pseudoindex is the least anticanonical degree of a rational curve.
