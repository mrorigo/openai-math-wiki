# 037. The ordinary-double-point volume gap

> Discipline: [Algebraic and complex geometry](../disciplines/algebraic-and-complex-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

## Description

Proves the ordinary-double-point volume-gap conjecture: every singular complex algebraic klt germ of dimension <i>n</i> ≥ 2, with zero boundary, has normalized volume at most $`2(n-1)^n`$. Equality holds precisely for an analytic ordinary double point.
