# 028. Uniformly bounded components of Gaussian-prime graphs

> Discipline: [Number theory](../disciplines/number-theory.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/028.md)

## Description

Proves the Gaussian moat conjecture: no infinite walk through distinct Gaussian primes can have uniformly bounded steps. More strongly, for every distance bound <i>D</i>, the graph joining Gaussian primes at distance at most <i>D</i> has uniformly bounded finite component sizes, depending only on <i>D</i>, including primes on the coordinate axes.
