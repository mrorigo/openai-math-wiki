# 108. A cubic permanent–determinant lower bound

> Discipline: [Theoretical computer science](../disciplines/theoretical-computer-science.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/108.md)

## Description

Proves an $`\Omega(n^3)`$ lower bound for the border determinantal complexity of the $`n\times n`$ permanent over ℂ. Even coefficientwise limits of determinants of affine-linear matrices require matrix size at least $`cn^3`$, for an absolute <i>c</i> &gt; 0 and all sufficiently large <i>n</i>; the same bound therefore holds for exact representations.
