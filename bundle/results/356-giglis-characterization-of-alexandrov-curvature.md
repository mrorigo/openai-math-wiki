# 356. Gigli’s characterization of Alexandrov curvature

> Discipline: [Differential geometry](../disciplines/differential-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/356.md)

## Description

Proves Gigli's conjecture: in every integer dimension <i>n</i> ≥ 2, Alexandrov curvature at least <i>κ</i> is characterized by the full-support $`\mathop{\mathrm{RCD}}\nolimits ((n-1)\kappa,n)`$ condition with reference measure $`\mathcal H^n`$ and distributional sectional curvature at least <i>κ</i> in the original global test classes. The RCD condition is unreduced.
