# 097. The Euclidean Steinitz–Bergström bound

> Discipline: [Convex and metric geometry](../disciplines/convex-and-metric-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/097.md)

## Description

Proves that any finite sequence in the Euclidean unit ball of ℝ<sup><i>d</i></sup> admits signs keeping every partial sum within $`C\sqrt d`$, independently of length. Consequently every zero-sum family can be reordered with the same bound on unsigned partial sums. A matching lower bound gives the optimal order $`S_2(d)=\Theta(\sqrt d)`$.
