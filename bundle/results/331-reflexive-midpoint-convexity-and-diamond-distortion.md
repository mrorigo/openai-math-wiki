# 331. Reflexive midpoint convexity and diamond distortion

> Discipline: [Functional analysis](../disciplines/functional-analysis.md)
> Manuscripts: **7**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/331.md)

## Description

Constructs a real reflexive Banach space with an asymptotically midpoint uniformly convex norm but no equivalent asymptotically uniformly convex norm, extending Baudier's separation to reflexive spaces. In the same space, depth-<i>k</i> countably branching diamonds require distortion at least $`\sqrt{1+k/12}`$, so midpoint uniform convexity does not force uniformly bounded diamond distortion even under reflexivity.
