# 296. The generator problem for finite factors

> Discipline: [Operator algebras](../disciplines/operator-algebras.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/296.md)

## Description

Proves that every type II<sub>1</sub> factor with separable predual is generated by a single operator, equivalently by two self-adjoint operators, resolving the generator problem. More strongly, for every irreducible inclusion $`P\subset M`$ of such factors, the unitaries <i>u</i> with $`M=W^*(P,u)`$ form a dense <i>G</i><sub><i>δ</i></sub> subset in the trace 2-norm topology.
