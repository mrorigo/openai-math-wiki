# 079. Local smoothing in three dimensions

> Discipline: [Real and complex analysis](../disciplines/real-and-complex-analysis.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Resolves Sogge's local smoothing conjecture for the Euclidean wave equation in three spatial dimensions. The estimate holds throughout the full strict range $`2\lt p\lt \infty`$, with Sobolev regularity above $`\max\{0,1-3/p\}`$. In particular, the critical <i>L</i><sup>3</sup> estimate holds with every positive Sobolev loss.
