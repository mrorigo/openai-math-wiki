# 160. Superexponential van der Waerden numbers

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/160.md)

## Description

Resolves Erdős's superexponential-growth question for van der Waerden numbers. If $`W_r(k)`$ is the least interval length forcing a monochromatic <i>k</i>-term progression in every <i>r</i>-coloring, then $`W_r(k)\gt k^{ck\lfloor\log_2 r\rfloor}`$ for an absolute <i>c</i> &gt; 0, all <i>r</i> ≥ 2 and sufficiently large <i>k</i>, uniformly in <i>r</i>. In particular, $`W_r(k)^{1/k}\to\infty`$ for each fixed <i>r</i>.
