# 159. Erdős’s reciprocal-sum conjecture and quasipolynomial Szemerédi bounds

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/159.md)  ·  [Abridged reasoning summary](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/reasoning_traces/quasipolynomial-arithmetic-progressions.pdf)

## Description

Proves Erdős's conjecture that every set of positive integers with divergent reciprocal sum contains arithmetic progressions of every finite length. Quantitatively, for each fixed <i>k</i> ≥ 3, every subset of $`\{1,\ldots,N\}`$ with no nonconstant <i>k</i>-term progression has size at most $`C_kN\exp[-c_k(\log N)^{\varepsilon_k}]`$, with positive constants depending only on <i>k</i>.
