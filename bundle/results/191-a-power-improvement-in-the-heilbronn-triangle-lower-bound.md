# 191. A power improvement in the Heilbronn triangle lower bound

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/191.md)

## Description

For every sufficiently large <i>n</i>, constructs <i>n</i> points in the unit square such that every triangle has area at least $`n^{-2+c}`$ for one absolute <i>c</i> &gt; 0. This disproves the conjectured almost-<i>n</i><sup>−2</sup> upper bound in Heilbronn's triangle problem, which asks how large the smallest determined triangle can be.
