# 078. The three-dimensional Bochner–Riesz conjecture

> Discipline: [Real and complex analysis](../disciplines/real-and-complex-analysis.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Resolves the three-dimensional Bochner–Riesz conjecture in its strict range: the Bochner–Riesz multipliers of order <i>δ</i> are bounded on $`L^p(\mathbb R^3)`$ for every $`1\le p\le\infty`$ whenever $`\delta\gt \max\{3|1/p-1/2|-1/2,0\}`$.
