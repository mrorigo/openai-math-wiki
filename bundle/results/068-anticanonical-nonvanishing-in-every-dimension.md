# 068. Anticanonical nonvanishing in every dimension

> Discipline: [Algebraic and complex geometry](../disciplines/algebraic-and-complex-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **7**

## Description

If <i>X</i> is a smooth connected complex projective variety and $`-K_X`$ admits a smooth Hermitian metric with nonnegative curvature, then $`H^0(X,-mK_X)\ne0`$ for some <i>m</i> &gt; 0. Thus smooth semipositivity forces a nonzero section of a positive tensor power of the anticanonical bundle in every dimension.
