# 133. The computational complexity of Weisfeiler–Leman refinement

> Discipline: [Theoretical computer science](../disciplines/theoretical-computer-science.md)
> Manuscripts: **4**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/133.md)

## Description

Proves unconditional $`n^{\Omega(k)}`$ deterministic time lower bounds for joint and separate <i>k</i>-dimensional Weisfeiler–Leman equivalence, for sufficiently large fixed <i>k</i> in the specified sequential adjacency-matrix models. With dimension as input, joint equivalence is EXPTIME-complete even on subcubic graphs; deciding whether refinement identifies a graph is also EXPTIME-complete.
