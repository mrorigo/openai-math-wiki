# 236. The exact factor-of-IID threshold for free Ising spins on trees

> Discipline: [Probability and statistical mechanics](../disciplines/probability-and-statistical-mechanics.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/236.md)

## Description

Determines when the free zero-field ferromagnetic Ising state on the infinite <i>d</i>-regular tree is a factor of independent vertex labels: exactly when $`\tanh\beta\le(d-1)^{-1/2}`$, including equality, for <i>d</i> ≥ 3 and <i>β</i> ≥ 0. The construction uses no root and is almost surely equivariant for each fixed tree automorphism, resolving the ferromagnetic case of Lyons's question.
