# 043. <i>P</i> = <i>W</i> for fixed-determinant SL<sub><i>n</i></sub> moduli spaces

> Discipline: [Algebraic and complex geometry](../disciplines/algebraic-and-complex-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Proves $`P_k=W_{2k}=W_{2k+1}`$ on the full rational cohomology of smooth coprime fixed-determinant, trace-free Higgs moduli spaces and their character varieties for composite ranks over smooth projective complex curves of genus at least two. This includes variant cohomology and, together with the known prime-rank theorems, establishes the fixed-determinant <i>P</i> = <i>W</i> conjecture in every coprime rank.
