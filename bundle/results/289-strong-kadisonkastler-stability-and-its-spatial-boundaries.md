# 289. Strong Kadison–Kastler stability and its spatial boundaries

> Discipline: [Operator algebras](../disciplines/operator-algebras.md)
> Manuscripts: **3**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/289.md)

## Description

Proves that sufficiently close unital von Neumann algebras on the same Hilbert space are conjugate by a unitary arbitrarily close to the identity, with a universal tolerance in the operator-norm distance between unit balls. Counterexamples show that near-identity conjugacy fails for one-sided near inclusions, and that arbitrarily close norm-separable <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebras need not be ambiently unitarily conjugate.
