# 089. Bounded-distortion <i>L</i><sub>1</sub> embeddings of planar and bounded-treewidth graphs

> Discipline: [Convex and metric geometry](../disciplines/convex-and-metric-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/089.md)

## Description

Resolves the planar and bounded-treewidth cases of the Gupta–Newman–Rabinovich–Sinclair conjecture. Shortest-path metrics of finite connected graphs with arbitrary positive edge lengths embed into real <i>L</i><sub>1</sub> with universal distortion for planar graphs, and distortion depending only on treewidth for bounded-treewidth graphs. The corresponding multicommodity flow–cut gaps are uniformly bounded.
