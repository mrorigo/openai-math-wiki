# 186. Uniform influence and sharp thresholds for graph and hypergraph properties

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/186.md)

## Description

Proves the Friedgut–Kalai threshold-width conjectures for graphs and fixed-uniformity hypergraphs. For fixed $`0\lt \varepsilon\lt 1/2`$, every nontrivial increasing relabeling-invariant property crosses from probability <i>ε</i> to $`1-\varepsilon`$ within width $`O((\log n)^{-2})`$ for graphs and $`O_r((\log n)^{-r/(r-1)})`$ for <i>r</i>-uniform hypergraphs, <i>r</i> ≥ 3. The hypergraph influence bound also applies to nonmonotone properties.
