# 325. The complete Crouzeix conjecture

> Discipline: [Functional analysis](../disciplines/functional-analysis.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/325.md)

## Description

Resolves the complete Crouzeix conjecture: for every bounded operator <i>A</i> on a complex Hilbert space and every finite matrix-valued polynomial <i>P</i>, one has $`\lVert P[A]\rVert\le2\sup_{z\in W(A)}\lVert P(z)\rVert`$, where $`W(A)`$ is the numerical range. The constant 2 is sharp, independent of the matrix size, and valid in infinite dimensions.
