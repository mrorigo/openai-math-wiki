# 348. Nonnegative-curvature Einstein classification and an <i>L</i><sup>2</sup> topological gap

> Discipline: [Differential geometry](../disciplines/differential-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **3**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/348.md)

## Description

Classifies closed connected Einstein four-manifolds with positive Einstein constant and nonnegative sectional curvature: up to scaling, their universal Riemannian covers are the round <i>S</i><sup>4</sup>, Fubini–Study $`\mathbb{CP}^2`$, or a product of equal round two-spheres. A closed simply connected nonnegatively curved four-manifold is diffeomorphic to one of these whenever the scale-invariant <i>L</i><sup>2</sup> norm of its trace-free Ricci curvature lies below a universal positive constant.
