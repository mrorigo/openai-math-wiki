# 375. De Giorgi's conjecture in dimension eight

> Discipline: [Partial differential equations](../disciplines/partial-differential-equations.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Proves De Giorgi's conjecture at its sharp dimension-eight endpoint: every entire <i>C</i><sup>2</sup> solution $`u:\mathbb R^8\to(-1,1)`$ of $`\Delta u=u^3-u`$ that is strictly increasing in one direction depends on only one linear coordinate. A stronger theorem classifies all stable entire solutions $`v:\mathbb R^7\to[-1,1]`$ as constant wells or planar transitions, without an energy-growth assumption.
