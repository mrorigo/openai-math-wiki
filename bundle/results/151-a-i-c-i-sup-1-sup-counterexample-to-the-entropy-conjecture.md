# 151. A <i>C</i><sup>1</sup> counterexample to the entropy conjecture

> Discipline: [Dynamical systems and ergodic theory](../disciplines/dynamical-systems-and-ergodic-theory.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/151.md)

## Description

Constructs a noninvertible <i>C</i><sup>1</sup> self-map of a compact smooth manifold with zero topological entropy but eigenvalue 2 on second homology. This disproves the homological entropy lower bound for general <i>C</i><sup>1</sup> self-maps: homological growth need not force positive orbit complexity.
