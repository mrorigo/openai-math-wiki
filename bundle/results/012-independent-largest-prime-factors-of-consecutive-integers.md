# 012. Independent largest prime factors of consecutive integers

> Discipline: [Number theory](../disciplines/number-theory.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/012.md)

## Description

Resolves the Erdős–Pomerance joint Dickman conjecture: the logarithmic sizes of the largest prime factors of <i>n</i> and $`n+1`$ are asymptotically independent in ordinary natural density. In particular, the integers satisfying $`P^+(n)\lt P^+(n+1)`$ have density 1/2.
