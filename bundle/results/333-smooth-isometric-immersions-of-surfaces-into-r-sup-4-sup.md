# 333. Smooth isometric immersions of surfaces into ℝ<sup>4</sup>

> Discipline: [Differential geometry](../disciplines/differential-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/333.md)

## Description

Every closed smooth Riemannian surface admits a smooth isometric immersion into ℝ<sup>4</sup>, resolving the closed-surface form of the four-dimensional isometric-immersion problem. This includes nonorientable surfaces and metrics of arbitrary Gaussian curvature.
