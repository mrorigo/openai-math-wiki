# 343. Symplectic ball packing in higher dimensions

> Discipline: [Differential geometry](../disciplines/differential-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/343.md)

## Description

Resolves the Siegel–Yao conjecture for arbitrary capacities in every dimension $`2n\ge6`$. Finitely many closed symplectic balls of capacities $`R_1,\ldots,R_k`$ embed disjointly into an open ball of capacity <i>R</i> exactly when $`\sum_iR_i^n\lt R^n`$ and $`R_i+R_j\lt R`$ for every distinct pair <i>i</i>, <i>j</i>.
