# 150. Weak mixing of triangular billiards with an irrational angle

> Discipline: [Dynamical systems and ergodic theory](../disciplines/dynamical-systems-and-ergodic-theory.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/150.md)

## Description

Proves that the billiard flow in every nondegenerate Euclidean triangle with at least one angle irrational relative to <i>π</i> is weakly mixing for normalized area times uniform direction. This strengthens ergodicity on the entire irrational-angle class, with no genericity or Diophantine restrictions.
