# 350. Yau’s nodal bounds: surfaces and higher dimensions

> Discipline: [Differential geometry](../disciplines/differential-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **3**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/350.md)

## Description

Proves the sharp $`C\sqrt\lambda`$ upper bound for nodal length on every fixed smooth closed surface, completing Yau's conjecture there. The upper bound fails for fixed smooth metrics in dimensions three and four, including metrics on <i>S</i><sup>3</sup> arbitrarily close to round. In dimension five, nodal measure can grow faster than $`\lambda^{1/2+\varepsilon_0}`$ for some fixed $`\varepsilon_0\gt 0`$, ruling out even arbitrarily small power losses.
