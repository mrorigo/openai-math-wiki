# 196. A counterexample to Kaplansky’s zero-divisor conjecture

> Discipline: [Algebra](../disciplines/algebra.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/196.md)

## Description

Constructs a finitely presented torsion-free group <i>G</i> whose group algebra $`\mathbb F_2[G]`$ has nonzero zero divisors, disproving Kaplansky's zero-divisor conjecture. The group has a finite two-dimensional classifying space.
