# 355. Unique tangent flows at the first surface singularity

> Discipline: [Differential geometry](../disciplines/differential-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Proves the first-singular-time case of tangent-flow uniqueness for smooth compact connected embedded surfaces without boundary in ℝ<sup>3</sup>. At every singular point, all fixed-center backward tangent flows agree as area measures at every negative time in the original ambient coordinates, without mean-convexity or a prescribed tangent model.
