# 359. Negative Kähler curvature without bounded holomorphic coordinates

> Discipline: [Differential geometry](../disciplines/differential-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/359.md)

## Description

Constructs a contractible domain in ℂ<sup>3</sup> with a complete negatively pinched Kähler metric but no bounded holomorphic coordinates, disproving bounded-domain uniformization in this setting. A higher-dimensional example has sectional curvature at most −1 and only constant bounded holomorphic functions; its curvature is not bounded below.
