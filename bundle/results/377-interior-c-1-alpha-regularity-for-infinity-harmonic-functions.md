# 377. Interior $`C^{1,\alpha}`$ regularity for infinity-harmonic functions

> Discipline: [Partial differential equations](../disciplines/partial-differential-equations.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Proves uniform interior $`C^{1,\alpha_d}`$ regularity for bounded infinity-harmonic functions in every dimension <i>d</i> ≥ 3, for a positive exponent depending only on dimension. The gradient's supremum norm and Hölder seminorm on the half unit ball are bounded by a dimension-dependent constant times the oscillation on the unit ball. The exponent is not explicit.
