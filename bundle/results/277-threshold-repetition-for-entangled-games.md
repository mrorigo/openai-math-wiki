# 277. Threshold repetition for entangled games

> Discipline: [Mathematical physics](../disciplines/mathematical-physics.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/277.md)

## Description

Proves exponential threshold repetition for every finite two-player one-round game: if its entangled value is <i>v</i> &lt; 1, the probability of winning at least a fraction $`v+\delta`$ of <i>k</i> independent repetitions decays exponentially in <i>k</i>, for $`0\lt \delta\lt 1-v`$. Arbitrary joint finite-dimensional entangled strategies and correlated question distributions are allowed.
