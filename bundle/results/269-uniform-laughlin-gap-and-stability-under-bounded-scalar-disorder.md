# 269. Uniform Laughlin gap and stability under bounded scalar disorder

> Discipline: [Mathematical physics](../disciplines/mathematical-physics.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/269.md)

## Description

Proves the fermionic Laughlin spectral-gap conjecture for the full <i>V</i><sub>1</sub> interaction at filling 1/3 on the round sphere. The unique ground state remains uniformly gapped under sufficiently weak bounded real scalar one-body potentials projected to the lowest Landau level. Both the gap and disorder threshold are uniform over all sufficiently large particle numbers and all normalized potential profiles.
