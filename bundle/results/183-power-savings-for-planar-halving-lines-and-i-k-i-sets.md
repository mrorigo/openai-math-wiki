# 183. Power savings for planar halving lines and <i>k</i>-sets

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/183.md)

## Description

Improves the planar halving-line bound to $`O(n^{4/3-\varepsilon})`$ for sets with no three collinear and an absolute <i>ε</i> &gt; 0. More generally, an <i>n</i>-point set with no three collinear has $`O(n(k+1)^{1/3-\varepsilon_0})`$ strictly separable <i>k</i>-subsets for $`1\le k\le n/2`$, with an absolute $`\varepsilon_0\gt 0`$. The constants and positive exponents are nonquantitative.
