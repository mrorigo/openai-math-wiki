# 288. Kadison's similarity conjecture

> Discipline: [Operator algebras](../disciplines/operator-algebras.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/288.md)

## Description

Proves Kadison's similarity conjecture: every bounded complex-linear unital algebra homomorphism from a unital complex <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebra to operators on a Hilbert space becomes a $`*`$-homomorphism after conjugation by a bounded invertible operator.
