# 246. Cannon's conjecture

> Discipline: [Group theory](../disciplines/group-theory.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/246.md)

## Description

Every word-hyperbolic group with boundary homeomorphic to <i>S</i><sup>2</sup> admits a proper cocompact isometric action on hyperbolic three-space with finite kernel, proving Cannon's conjecture. Every torsion-free such group is therefore the fundamental group of a closed hyperbolic three-manifold.
