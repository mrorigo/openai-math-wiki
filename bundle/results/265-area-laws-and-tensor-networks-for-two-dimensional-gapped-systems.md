# 265. Area laws and tensor networks for two-dimensional gapped systems

> Discipline: [Mathematical physics](../disciplines/mathematical-physics.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

## Description

Proves an entropy area law for unique ground states of finite-range Hamiltonians on arbitrary finite induced square-lattice domains, using only a uniform full-system spectral gap and bounds on the local interactions. On open $`L\times L`$ squares, uniformly gapped nearest-neighbor ground states also admit projected entangled-pair state approximations with polynomial bond dimension and global vector error at most <i>L</i><sup>−1</sup>.
