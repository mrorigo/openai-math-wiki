# 166. The higher-dimensional Erdős distinct-distances conjecture

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

For every fixed <i>d</i> ≥ 3, any <i>n</i> ≥ 2 distinct points in ℝ<sup><i>d</i></sup> determine at least $`c_dn^{2/d}`$ distinct distances, with $`c_d\gt 0`$ depending only on dimension. This matches the integer-grid order and resolves the higher-dimensional Erdős distinct-distances conjecture with a constant-factor bound.
