# 188. The sharp terminal leave in random triangle removal

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/188.md)

## Description

Starting from the complete graph on <i>n</i> vertices, repeatedly delete a uniformly chosen remaining triangle. The terminal edge count is asymptotic to $`n^{3/2}/(2\sqrt2)`$, with mean-square convergence after normalization by <i>n</i><sup>3/2</sup>. This proves the triangle case of the Joos–Kühn sharp-constant conjecture.
