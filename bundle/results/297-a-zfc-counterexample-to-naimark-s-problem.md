# 297. A ZFC counterexample to Naimark's problem

> Discipline: [Operator algebras](../disciplines/operator-algebras.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/297.md)

## Description

Gives an alternative to [Tanaka's ZFC construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26930v1) of a unital infinite-dimensional simple complex <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebra with a faithful tracial state and exactly one nonzero irreducible representation up to unitary equivalence. Thus the unrestricted compact-operator characterization fails without additional set-theoretic assumptions; the counterexample is nonseparable.
