# 345. Infinitely many closed geodesics on Riemannian spheres and closed three-manifolds

> Discipline: [Differential geometry](../disciplines/differential-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Proves that every smooth Riemannian metric on <i>S</i><sup><i>n</i></sup>, <i>n</i> ≥ 2, has infinitely many prime closed geodesics with pairwise distinct images. The same conclusion holds on every closed manifold admitting a finite smooth spherical cover and on every closed three-manifold, without orientability or nondegeneracy restrictions.
