# 040. Bloch’s conjecture for complex surfaces

> Discipline: [Algebraic and complex geometry](../disciplines/algebraic-and-complex-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Proves Bloch's conjecture: for every smooth connected projective complex surface <i>S</i> with $`p_g(S)=0`$, the Albanese map $`\mathrm{CH}_0(S)^0\to\mathrm{Alb}(S)(\mathbb C)`$ on integral degree-zero zero-cycles is an isomorphism. This combines the new $`p_g=q=0`$ theorem with the classical theorem of Bloch, Kas, and Lieberman.
