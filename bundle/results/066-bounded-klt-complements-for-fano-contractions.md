# 066. Bounded klt complements for Fano contractions

> Discipline: [Algebraic and complex geometry](../disciplines/algebraic-and-complex-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/066.md)

## Description

Proves the finite-rational-coefficient form of Shokurov’s bounded-klt-complement conjecture for <i>ϵ</i>-lc complex Fano-type pairs with nef anti-log-canonical divisor. For <i>ϵ</i>-lc Fano contractions over any algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, it gives klt complements near every base point, with index bounded only by dimension and positive rational <i>ϵ</i>.
