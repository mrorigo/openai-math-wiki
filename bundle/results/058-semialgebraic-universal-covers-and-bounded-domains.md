# 058. Semialgebraic universal covers and bounded domains

> Discipline: [Algebraic and complex geometry](../disciplines/algebraic-and-complex-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/058.md)

## Description

Proves the Kollár–Pardon conjecture: the semialgebraic universal covers of connected normal projective complex varieties are exactly products $`D\times\mathbb C^m\times F`$, with <i>D</i> bounded symmetric and <i>F</i> simply connected, normal, and projective. A universal cover is quasi-projective exactly when the bounded symmetric factor is absent. In particular, a smooth projective variety covered by ℂ<sup><i>n</i></sup> has a finite étale cover by an abelian variety.
