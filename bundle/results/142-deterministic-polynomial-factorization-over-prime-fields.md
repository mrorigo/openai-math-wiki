# 142. Deterministic polynomial factorization over prime fields

> Discipline: [Theoretical computer science](../disciplines/theoretical-computer-science.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Gives a uniform deterministic algorithm that completely factors every nonzero dense degree-<i>n</i> polynomial over a prime field 𝔽<sub><i>p</i></sub>, including multiplicities, in bit complexity polynomial in $`(n+1)\log p`$. The prime is supplied in binary. No randomness, integer-factorization or primitive-root oracle, or GRH assumption is required.
