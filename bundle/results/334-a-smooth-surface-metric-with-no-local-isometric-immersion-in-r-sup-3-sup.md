# 334. A smooth surface metric with no local isometric immersion in ℝ<sup>3</sup>

> Discipline: [Differential geometry](../disciplines/differential-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/334.md)

## Description

Constructs a smooth positive-definite metric on $`(-1,1)^2`$ for which no neighborhood of the origin admits a smooth isometric immersion into ℝ<sup>3</sup>. This answers the unrestricted smooth local isometric realization problem for surfaces negatively, even after shrinking the neighborhood.
