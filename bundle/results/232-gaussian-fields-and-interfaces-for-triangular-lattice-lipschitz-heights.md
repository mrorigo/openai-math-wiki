# 232. Gaussian fields and interfaces for triangular-lattice Lipschitz heights

> Discipline: [Probability and statistical mechanics](../disciplines/probability-and-statistical-mechanics.md)
> Manuscripts: **3**

## Description

Proves Gaussian free field limits on bounded smooth simply connected domains for triangular-lattice height models: uniform odd heights with increments $`0,\pm2`$ and two-arc boundary values $`\pm1`$, and zero-boundary integer Lipschitz heights weighted by fixed $`x\in[1/\sqrt2,1]`$. Uniform real Lipschitz heights also converge to a Gaussian field; at a tuned opposite-boundary amplitude, their interface converges to chordal SLE<sub>4</sub>, establishing Schramm’s real-field/interface predictions.
