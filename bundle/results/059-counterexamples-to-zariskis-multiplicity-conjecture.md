# 059. Counterexamples to Zariski’s multiplicity conjecture

> Discipline: [Algebraic and complex geometry](../disciplines/algebraic-and-complex-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

## Description

Disproves Zariski's multiplicity conjecture by constructing reduced holomorphic hypersurface germs that are ambiently homeomorphic but have different multiplicities. The examples include hypersurfaces in ℂ<sup>4</sup> with isolated critical points and multiplicities four and five.
