# 094. Subpolynomial dimension reduction in <i>L</i><sub><i>p</i></sub>

> Discipline: [Convex and metric geometry](../disciplines/convex-and-metric-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/094.md)

## Description

For every fixed $`1\lt p\lt \infty`$ and distortion <i>D</i> &gt; 1, every <i>n</i>-point subset of real <i>L</i><sub><i>p</i></sub> embeds into $`\ell_p^d`$ with distortion at most <i>D</i> and dimension $`d=n^{o(1)}`$, answering Naor's sublinear-dimension question for <i>p</i> ≠ 2. In contrast, exact embeddings require worst-case dimension $`\Theta(n^2)`$ when <i>p</i> ≠ 2.
