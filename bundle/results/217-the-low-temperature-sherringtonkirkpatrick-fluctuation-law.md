# 217. The low-temperature Sherrington–Kirkpatrick fluctuation law

> Discipline: [Probability and statistical mechanics](../disciplines/probability-and-statistical-mechanics.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

## Description

For every fixed inverse temperature <i>β</i> &gt; 1, determines the fluctuation scale and limiting law of the zero-field Gaussian Sherrington–Kirkpatrick log partition function. Its variance is asymptotic to $`c_\beta n^{1/3}`$, with $`c_\beta\gt 0`$, confirming the predicted <i>n</i><sup>1/6</sup> standard-deviation scale. Exact centering and standardization give full-sequence convergence to a uniquely characterized nondegenerate law.
