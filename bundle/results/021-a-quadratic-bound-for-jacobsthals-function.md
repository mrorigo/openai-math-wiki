# 021. A quadratic bound for Jacobsthal’s function

> Discipline: [Number theory](../disciplines/number-theory.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/021.md)

## Description

Answers Jacobsthal's quadratic-bound question: every interval of $`Ck^2`$ consecutive integers contains an integer coprime to any prescribed positive integer with at most <i>k</i> distinct prime divisors, for an absolute constant <i>C</i>. The bound is uniform over prime sets and interval positions and removes the classical logarithmic loss.
