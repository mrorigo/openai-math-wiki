# 157. Graph coloring, clique minors, and Colin de Verdière invariants

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **3**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/157.md)

## Description

Disproves Hadwiger's conjecture even for fractional coloring: arbitrarily large finite simple graphs with independence number at most two satisfy $`\chi_f(G)\gt h(G)`$, where $`h(G)`$ is the largest clique-minor order. Also disproves the fractional Colin de Verdière chromatic bound $`\chi_f(G)\le\mu(G)+1`$. In the positive direction, every finite nonempty graph satisfies $`\chi_{\mathrm{list}}(G)\le C h(G)`$ for a universal constant <i>C</i>.
