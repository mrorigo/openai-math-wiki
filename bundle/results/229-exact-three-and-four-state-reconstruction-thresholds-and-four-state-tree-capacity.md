# 229. Exact three- and four-state reconstruction thresholds and four-state tree capacity

> Discipline: [Probability and statistical mechanics](../disciplines/probability-and-statistical-mechanics.md)
> Manuscripts: **3**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/229.md)

## Description

Proves the exact reconstruction threshold $`d\lambda^2\gt 1`$, with nonreconstruction at equality, for three-state symmetric and four-state ferromagnetic broadcasting on regular trees (<i>d</i> ≥ 2) and observed Poisson trees (mean <i>d</i> &gt; 1 and <i>d</i> &gt; 0, respectively), with Poisson advantage averaged without conditioning on survival. The three-state theorem allows both signs of <i>λ</i> and gives the exact weak-recovery threshold for the symmetric three-community stochastic block model.
