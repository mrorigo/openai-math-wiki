# 219. GOE bulk universality for regular graphs with weak Anderson disorder

> Discipline: [Probability and statistical mechanics](../disciplines/probability-and-statistical-mechanics.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

## Description

For every fixed degree <i>d</i> ≥ 3, the bulk adjacency-eigenvalue point process of a uniform simple random <i>d</i>-regular graph converges to the Gaussian orthogonal ensemble law, including for cubic graphs. The same fixed-energy universality persists under sufficiently weak fixed iid uniform diagonal disorder, throughout compact bands strictly inside the clean spectral edges.
