# 019. The local <i>p</i>-adic section conjecture and global consequences

> Discipline: [Number theory](../disciplines/number-theory.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

## Description

Proves that rational points on every smooth proper geometrically connected curve of genus at least two over a finite extension of ℚ<sub><i>p</i></sub> correspond bijectively to conjugacy classes of sections of its full arithmetic étale fundamental group. It also proves Grothendieck's section conjecture over ℚ for the modular curves $`X_0(N)`$ and $`X_1(N)`$ of genus at least two.
