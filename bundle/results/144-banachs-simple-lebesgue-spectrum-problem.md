# 144. Banach’s simple Lebesgue-spectrum problem

> Discipline: [Dynamical systems and ergodic theory](../disciplines/dynamical-systems-and-ergodic-theory.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/144.md)

## Description

Resolves the probability-preserving form of Banach's simple Lebesgue-spectrum problem within smooth dynamics. A smooth volume-preserving diffeomorphism of the standard-volume three-torus has simple Lebesgue spectrum on its entire complex mean-zero <i>L</i><sup>2</sup> space: the bilateral iterates of one real observable form an orthonormal basis of that space.
