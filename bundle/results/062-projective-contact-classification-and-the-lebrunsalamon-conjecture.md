# 062. Projective contact classification and the LeBrun–Salamon conjecture

> Discipline: [Algebraic and complex geometry](../disciplines/algebraic-and-complex-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Proves the LeBrun–Salamon conjecture: every closed connected positive quaternionic-Kähler manifold of real dimension at least eight is homothetic to a compact symmetric Wolf space. It also proves contact-Fano homogeneity and classifies smooth connected complex projective contact manifolds of complex dimension at least three: those with $`b_2=1`$ are adjoint varieties with their canonical contact structures, while those with $`b_2\ge2`$ have underlying manifold $`\mathbb P(T^*Z)`$ for a smooth projective variety <i>Z</i>.
