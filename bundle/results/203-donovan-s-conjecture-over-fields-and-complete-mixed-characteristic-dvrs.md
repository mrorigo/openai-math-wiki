# 203. Donovan's conjecture over fields and complete mixed-characteristic DVRs

> Discipline: [Algebra](../disciplines/algebra.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

## Description

Proves Donovan's conjecture: over each fixed algebraically closed field of characteristic <i>p</i>, blocks of finite groups with bounded defect-group order have only finitely many Morita-equivalence classes, for every prime <i>p</i>. Also proves the integral form over each fixed complete mixed-characteristic discrete valuation ring with algebraically closed residue field.
