# 302. Radius of comparison equals half the mean dimension

> Discipline: [Operator algebras](../disciplines/operator-algebras.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

## Description

For every minimal homeomorphism <i>h</i> of an infinite compact metrizable space <i>X</i>, the radius of comparison of $`C(X)\rtimes_h\mathbb Z`$ equals $`\tfrac12\mathrm{mdim}(X,h)`$, including infinite values. Zero mean dimension is equivalent to the small boundary property, Jiang–Su stability and finite nuclear dimension; in this case nuclear dimension is at most one.
