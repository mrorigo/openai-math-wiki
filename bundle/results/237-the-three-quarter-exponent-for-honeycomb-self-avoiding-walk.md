# 237. The three-quarter exponent for honeycomb self-avoiding walk

> Discipline: [Probability and statistical mechanics](../disciplines/probability-and-statistical-mechanics.md)
> Manuscripts: **13**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/237.md)

## Description

Proves the diameter form of Nienhuis's predicted three-quarter exponent: a uniformly chosen <i>n</i>-step self-avoiding walk on the honeycomb lattice has diameter $`n^{3/4+o(1)}`$. Its local mass and covering numbers have exponent 4/3. These estimates hold at every sufficiently large fixed length, simultaneously across scales, with arbitrarily high polynomial probability.
