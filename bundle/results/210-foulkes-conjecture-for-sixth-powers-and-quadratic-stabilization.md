# 210. Foulkes' conjecture for sixth powers and quadratic stabilization

> Discipline: [Algebra](../disciplines/algebra.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/210.md)

## Description

Proves the sixth case of Foulkes’ conjecture: $`\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^6(\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^bV)`$ embeds equivariantly in $`\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^b(\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^6V)`$ for every <i>b</i> ≥ 6 and finite-dimensional complex <i>V</i>. More generally, the canonical multiplication map $`\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^b(\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^aV)\to\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^a(\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^bV)`$ is surjective for <i>a</i> ≥ 2 and $`b\ge a(a-1)`$, giving dimension-independent quadratic stabilization.
