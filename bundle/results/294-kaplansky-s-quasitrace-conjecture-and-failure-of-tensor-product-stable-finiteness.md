# 294. Kaplansky's quasitrace conjecture and failure of tensor-product stable finiteness

> Discipline: [Operator algebras](../disciplines/operator-algebras.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/294.md)

## Description

Disproves Kaplansky's quasitrace conjecture by constructing a separable unital complex <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebra admitting normalized 2-quasitraces, all of which are nonadditive. As a consequence, two unital simple stably finite <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebras can have a properly infinite minimal tensor product, with one factor $`C_r^*(\mathbb F_2)`$.
