# 152. Zero entropy does not guarantee a smooth positive-volume model

> Discipline: [Dynamical systems and ergodic theory](../disciplines/dynamical-systems-and-ergodic-theory.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/152.md)

## Description

Constructs a zero-entropy ergodic invertible transformation of a standard nonatomic probability space that is not measurably conjugate to any <i>C</i><sup>∞</sup> diffeomorphism preserving a strictly positive smooth probability density on a compact finite-dimensional manifold. One example rules out every finite dimension.
