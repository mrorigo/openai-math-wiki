# 225. Gaussian free field limits throughout the balanced six-vertex regime

> Discipline: [Probability and statistical mechanics](../disciplines/probability-and-statistical-mechanics.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

The balanced square-lattice six-vertex height field with $`a=b=1`$ and $`0\lt c\le2`$ converges to a Gaussian free field, including at the endpoint <i>c</i> = 2. The plane state is defined by balanced-torus limits. For unit height increments and Green kernel $`-(2\pi)^{-1}\log|x-y|`$, the exact variance multiplier is $`1/\arcsin(c/2)`$.
