# 326. The cotype–cotype conjecture under the approximation property

> Discipline: [Functional analysis](../disciplines/functional-analysis.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/326.md)

## Description

Resolves the cotype–cotype conjecture for real Banach spaces with the approximation property. Such a nonzero space is <i>K</i>-convex if and only if both it and its dual have finite Rademacher cotype, with possibly different exponents. Equivalently, these cotype assumptions force nontrivial Rademacher type.
