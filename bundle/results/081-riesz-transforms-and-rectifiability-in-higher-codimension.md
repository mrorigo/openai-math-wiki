# 081. Riesz transforms and rectifiability in higher codimension

> Discipline: [Real and complex analysis](../disciplines/real-and-complex-analysis.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/081.md)

## Description

Resolves the remaining higher-codimension Riesz-transform rectifiability problem: for <i>d</i> ≥ 4 and $`2\le n\le d-2`$, an <i>n</i>-Ahlfors–David regular Radon measure on ℝ<sup><i>d</i></sup> is uniformly <i>n</i>-rectifiable whenever its <i>n</i>-dimensional Riesz transform is uniformly <i>L</i><sup>2</sup>-bounded over all positive hard truncations. The rectifiability bounds depend only on dimension, regularity and operator bounds.
