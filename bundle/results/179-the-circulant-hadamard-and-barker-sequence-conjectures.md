# 179. The circulant Hadamard and Barker-sequence conjectures

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/179.md)

## Description

Proves that real circulant Hadamard matrices exist exactly in orders 1 and 4, resolving the circulant Hadamard conjecture. Together with classical Barker-sequence results, this shows that binary sequences whose nontrivial aperiodic autocorrelations have magnitude at most 1 exist at lengths <i>n</i> &gt; 1 exactly when $`n\in\{2,3,4,5,7,11,13\}`$.
