# 361. Failure of integer-degree harmonic dimension comparison

> Discipline: [Differential geometry](../disciplines/differential-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/361.md)

## Description

Disproves Yau's proposed Euclidean dimension bound for harmonic functions of integer growth on manifolds with nonnegative Ricci curvature. For every sufficiently large integer <i>k</i>, a complete smooth metric on ℝ<sup>3</sup> has at least $`(k+2)^2`$ independent harmonic functions of growth at most <i>k</i>, exceeding the Euclidean count $`(k+1)^2`$. The metric may depend on <i>k</i>.
