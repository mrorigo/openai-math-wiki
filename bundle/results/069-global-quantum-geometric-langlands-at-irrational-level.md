# 069. Global quantum geometric Langlands at irrational level

> Discipline: [Algebraic and complex geometry](../disciplines/algebraic-and-complex-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Proves the unramified de Rham quantum geometric Langlands equivalence for every connected simple complex algebraic group on every smooth projective connected complex curve, at every shifted level $`c\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb Q`$. It identifies the full derived categories of twisted <i>D</i>-modules for the group and its Langlands dual, retaining all global forms and connected components.
