# 162. Counterexamples to Ryser’s covering conjecture

> Discipline: [Combinatorics](../disciplines/combinatorics.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/162.md)

## Description

Disproves Ryser's covering conjecture by constructing intersecting $`(q+1)`$-partite, $`(q+1)`$-uniform hypergraphs with covering number $`q+1`$, rather than the predicted bound <i>q</i>, for every sufficiently large prime <i>q</i>. A separate construction over extension fields also disproves Gyárfás's monochromatic tree-cover conjecture.
