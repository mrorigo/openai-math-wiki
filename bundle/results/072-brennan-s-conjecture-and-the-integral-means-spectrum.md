# 072. Brennan's conjecture and the integral-means spectrum

> Discipline: [Real and complex analysis](../disciplines/real-and-complex-analysis.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/072.md)

## Description

Proves Brennan's conjecture: for every conformal bijection <i>ϕ</i> from a simply connected plane domain onto the disk, $`|\phi'|^s`$ is area-integrable for $`4/3\lt s\lt 4`$. The sharp universal integral-means identity is $`B_{\mathcal S}(t)=|t|-1`$ for <i>t</i> ≤ −2. A strict bound $`B_b(-1)\lt 1/4`$ for bounded univalent functions disproves Kraetzer's prediction at that parameter.
