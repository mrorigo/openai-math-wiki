# 303. Weak pure infiniteness and Cuntz-algebra absorption

> Discipline: [Operator algebras](../disciplines/operator-algebras.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/303.md)

## Description

Resolves the ordinary-to-strong pure-infiniteness question of Kirchberg and Rørdam for complex <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebras. For exact algebras, proper infiniteness of one fixed finite amplification of every positive element also suffices. Consequently, every separable nuclear algebra with this property absorbs $`\mathcal O_\infty`$, without unitality or simplicity assumptions.
