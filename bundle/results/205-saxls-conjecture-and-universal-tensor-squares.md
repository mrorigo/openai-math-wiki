# 205. Saxl’s conjecture and universal tensor squares

> Discipline: [Algebra](../disciplines/algebra.md)
> Manuscripts: **2**

[Lean formalization of headline result](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/205.md)

## Description

Proves Saxl's conjecture: the tensor square of every staircase representation contains every irreducible complex representation of the corresponding symmetric group. More generally, every <i>S</i><sub><i>n</i></sub> with $`n\notin\{2,4,9\}`$ has an irreducible representation whose tensor square contains all irreducibles.
