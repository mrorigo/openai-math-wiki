# 064. Topological triviality of <i>μ</i>-constant surface singularities

> Discipline: [Algebraic and complex geometry](../disciplines/algebraic-and-complex-geometry.md)
> Manuscripts: **1**

## Description

Proves topological right-triviality for every holomorphic one-parameter family of isolated hypersurface singularities in ℂ<sup>3</sup> with constant Milnor number, resolving the surface case of the <i>μ</i>-constant problem. After shrinking the parameter disk and representatives, ambient homeomorphisms vary jointly continuously, fix the origin section and preserve the defining functions.
