## Abstract

We prove a conjecture of Erdős: for every sufficiently large integer <i>b</i>, every rational number $`a/b`$ with $`1\le a\lt b`$ is a sum of $`O(\log\log b)`$ distinct positive unit fractions, with an absolute implied constant. This order is best possible when the numerator varies. We also show that both the number of expansions of 1 with exactly <i>k</i> distinct terms and the least integer at least 2 that never occurs as a denominator in such an expansion grow doubly exponentially in <i>k</i>: their double logarithms have order <i>k</i>.
