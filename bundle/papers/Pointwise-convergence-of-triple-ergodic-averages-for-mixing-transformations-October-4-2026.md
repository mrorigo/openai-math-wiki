## Abstract

We prove pointwise convergence of triple ergodic averages for every invertible mixing probability preserving transformation <i>T</i> of an arbitrary probability space $`(X,\mathcal F,\mu)`$, with measurable inverse. For every triple of bounded measurable functions $`f_1,f_2,f_3:X\to\mathbb C`$,

$`\displaystyle \frac1N\sum_{n=1}^N f_1(T^nx)f_2(T^{2n}x)f_3(T^{3n}x) \longrightarrow \prod_{j=1}^3\int_X f_j\,d\mu`$

for <i>μ</i>-almost every <i>x</i> as $`N\to\infty`$ through all positive integers.
