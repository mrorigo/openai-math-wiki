## Abstract

We derive quantitative distortion bounds for countably branching diamond graphs from midpoint estimates and direct tree energies. In the dual of a finite-height segment forest, every distortion-<i>D</i> embedding of the depth-<i>k</i> diamond satisfies $`D^2\ge1+k/4`$. The same bound holds in the infinite-height coordinate predual. We also obtain power-type distortion bounds for path-cost and recursive tree norms.
