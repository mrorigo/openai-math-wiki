## Abstract

We prove that every smooth connected complex projective Fano sixfold with nef tangent bundle is rational homogeneous, resolving the Campana–Peternell conjecture in complex dimension six. As a consequence, a connected compact Kähler manifold <i>X</i> with nef holomorphic tangent bundle and $`\dim_{\mathbb C}X-\widetilde q(X)\leq6`$ has ordinary universal cover $`F\times\mathbb C^{\widetilde q(X)}`$, where <i>F</i> is a rational homogeneous manifold and $`\widetilde q(X)`$ is the maximal irregularity of a connected finite étale cover.
