## Abstract

We prove the quasilinear-size PCP-for-PPAD conjecture of Babichenko, Papadimitriou, and Rubinstein. There are fixed positive rational constants <i>ε</i> and <i>δ</i> and a deterministic polynomial-time reduction that transforms an End-of-Line instance of binary length <i>N</i> into a generalized circuit of total binary length $`N(\log N)^{O(1)}`$. From any rational assignment of polynomial encoding length that <i>ε</i>-satisfies all but a <i>δ</i> fraction of the gates, a solution to the original End-of-Line instance can be recovered in polynomial time, regardless of which gates fail. Such assignments always exist, with one fixed polynomial bound on their encoding length.
