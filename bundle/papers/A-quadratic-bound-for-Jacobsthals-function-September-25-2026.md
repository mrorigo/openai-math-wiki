## Abstract

Let $`h(k)`$ be the least integer such that every interval of $`h(k)`$ consecutive integers contains an integer coprime to any prescribed positive integer having at most <i>k</i> distinct prime divisors. We prove $`h(k)\ll k^2/(\log\log(3k))^2`$, giving an affirmative answer to Jacobsthal's quadratic-bound question.
