## Abstract

We prove the unramified quantum geometric Langlands equivalence for every connected simple complex algebraic group <i>G</i> and every level $`c\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb Q`$, including non-real levels. For every smooth projective connected complex curve <i>X</i>, it identifies the full twisted <i>D</i>-module categories on $`\mathop{\mathrm{Bun}}\nolimits _G(X)`$ and $`\mathop{\mathrm{Bun}}\nolimits _{G^\vee}(X)`$ at the dual shifted levels <i>c</i> and $`-1/(rc)`$, where <i>r</i> is the lacing number. The equivalence uses the given global forms and includes all connected components.
