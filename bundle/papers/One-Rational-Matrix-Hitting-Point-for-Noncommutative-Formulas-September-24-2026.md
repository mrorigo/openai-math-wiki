## Abstract

We construct, in deterministic polynomial bit time, one tuple of rational matrices that detects every nonzero polynomial computed by a noncommutative division-free formula of a prescribed size. The matrices have dimension $`O(ns^2)`$ for <i>n</i> variables and formula size <i>s</i>, and the same tuple works over every field of characteristic zero.
