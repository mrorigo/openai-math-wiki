## Abstract

We resolve the three-dimensional Ball–Evans approximation problem for every finite <i>p</i> &gt; 2. Every $`W^{1,p}`$ homeomorphism between arbitrary bounded domains in ℝ<sup>3</sup> can be approximated strongly in $`W^{1,p}`$ by smooth diffeomorphisms onto the same target.
