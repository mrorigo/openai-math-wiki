## Abstract

We construct a single group of type <i>F</i><sub>∞</sub> containing every finitely presented group. Its finitely generated subgroups, up to isomorphism, are exactly the finitely generated recursively presented groups. This answers the <i>F</i><sub>∞</sub> form of the higher-dimensional Higman embedding question.
