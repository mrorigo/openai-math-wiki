Let <i>A</i><sub><i>n</i></sub> be a symmetric $`n\times n`$ matrix whose entries on and above the diagonal are independent uniform signs. We prove

$`\displaystyle \Pr(\det A_n=0)=\left(\frac12+o(1)\right)^n.`$
