## Abstract

Let $`\mathop{\mathrm{IMM}}\nolimits _{n,n}`$ be the $`(1,1)`$ entry of a product of <i>n</i> independent $`n\times n`$ matrices of variables. Over every field of characteristic zero, every syntactically homogeneous $`\Sigma\Pi\Sigma\Pi\Sigma`$ circuit computing $`\mathop{\mathrm{IMM}}\nolimits _{n,n}`$ has at least $`n^{\sqrt n/400}`$ gates for all sufficiently large <i>n</i>, with an absolute threshold independent of the field. Bottom linear forms may have arbitrary support, and arbitrary finite fan-in, fan-out, and gate sharing are allowed. Over every field, a block expansion gives such circuits with at most $`n^{\sqrt n+4}`$ gates for <i>n</i> ≥ 2. Thus the gate complexity over characteristic-zero fields is $`n^{\Theta(\sqrt n)}`$.
