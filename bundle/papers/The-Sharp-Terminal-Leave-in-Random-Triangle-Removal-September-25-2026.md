## Abstract

Starting from the complete graph on <i>n</i> vertices, repeatedly remove the three edges of a uniformly chosen remaining triangle. We prove that the number of edges left at termination, divided by <i>n</i><sup>3/2</sup>, converges in <i>L</i><sup>2</sup> to $`1/(2\sqrt2)`$. This proves the triangle case of the sharp-constant conjecture of Joos and Kühn. In particular, the same limit holds in probability and for the normalized expectation.
