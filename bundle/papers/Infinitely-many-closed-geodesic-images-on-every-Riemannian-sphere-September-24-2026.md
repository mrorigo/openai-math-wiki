## Abstract

We resolve the sphere case of the closed-geodesic infinitude problem: every smooth Riemannian metric on the standard sphere <i>S</i><sup><i>n</i></sup>, <i>n</i> ≥ 2, has infinitely many prime closed geodesics with pairwise distinct images. The two-sphere case is classical; the result in higher dimensions includes degenerate metrics. Using finite covers and theorems of Perelman and Rademacher–Taimanov, we also obtain the same conclusion for every smooth Riemannian metric on a nonempty closed smooth three-manifold.
