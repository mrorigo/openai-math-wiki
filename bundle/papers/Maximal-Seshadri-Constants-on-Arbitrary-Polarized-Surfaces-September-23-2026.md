## Abstract

We prove that, for every smooth integral complex projective surface <i>S</i> and every ample line bundle <i>L</i>, the multipoint Seshadri constant at <i>r</i> very general points equals $`\sqrt{L^2/r}`$ for every sufficiently large integer <i>r</i>. This resolves positively the qualitative Nagata–Biran conjecture for surfaces.
