## Abstract

We resolve the Falconer distance conjecture in every dimension. For every integer <i>d</i> ≥ 2, a compact subset of ℝ<sup><i>d</i></sup> with Hausdorff dimension greater than $`d/2`$ determines a set of Euclidean distances of positive Lebesgue measure.
