## Abstract

For random 3-SAT on <i>n</i> Boolean variables, with independent uniformly signed clauses on three distinct variables sampled with replacement, we prove that the first unsatisfiable prefix has variance $`\Theta(n)`$. The upper bound removes the logarithmic loss in the earlier variance estimate; the matching lower bound follows from Wilson's transition-width theorem.
