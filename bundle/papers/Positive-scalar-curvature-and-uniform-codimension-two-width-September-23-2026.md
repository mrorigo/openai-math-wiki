## Abstract

We prove the quantitative continuous form of Gromov's scalar-curvature conjecture in every dimension <i>n</i> ≥ 4. Every complete connected smooth boundaryless <i>n</i>-manifold with scalar curvature at least one admits a continuous map to a simplicial complex of dimension at most $`n-2`$ whose entire fibers have diameter bounded only in terms of <i>n</i>. We also obtain the continuous macroscopic-dimension conclusion for universal covers of closed manifolds with positive scalar curvature in every dimension <i>n</i> ≥ 2.
