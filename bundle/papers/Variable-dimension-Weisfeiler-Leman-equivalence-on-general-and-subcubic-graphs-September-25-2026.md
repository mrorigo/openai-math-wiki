## Abstract

Deciding joint-update <i>k</i>-dimensional Weisfeiler–Leman equivalence is $`\mathsf{EXPTIME}`$-complete when the two graphs are given by explicit adjacency matrices and <i>k</i> ≥ 2 is encoded in binary. The result holds even for connected simple uncolored graphs of equal positive order and maximum degree at most three.
