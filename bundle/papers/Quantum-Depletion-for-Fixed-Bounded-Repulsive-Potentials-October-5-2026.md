## Abstract

We prove the Bogoliubov quantum-depletion asymptotic for the ground state of a three-dimensional Bose gas with a fixed bounded, nonnegative, radial interaction of finite range and positive scattering length <i>a</i>. The thermodynamic limit is taken at each fixed density <i>ρ</i> before the dilute limit. Every thermodynamic accumulation value of the fraction outside the constant mode is $`\frac{8}{3\sqrt\pi}\sqrt{\rho a^3}+o(\sqrt{\rho a^3})`$ as $`\rho\downarrow0`$. The assertion is uniform over ground-state density matrices and does not require the occupation to have a unique thermodynamic limit.
