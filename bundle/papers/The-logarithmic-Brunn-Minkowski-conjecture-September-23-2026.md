## Abstract

We prove the logarithmic Brunn–Minkowski conjecture for arbitrary origin-symmetric convex bodies in every dimension. The theorem also gives the symmetric <i>L</i><sub><i>p</i></sub> Brunn–Minkowski inequality for every $`0\lt p\lt 1`$. Combined with Saroglou's transfer theorem and a support-subspace reduction, it yields the logarithmic inequality for every even log-concave Radon measure and the scalar-dilation $`(B)`$-conjecture.
