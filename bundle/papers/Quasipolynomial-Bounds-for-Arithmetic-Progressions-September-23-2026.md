## Abstract

We prove Erdős's conjecture that every set of positive integers with divergent reciprocal sum contains arithmetic progressions of every finite length. More quantitatively, for every fixed <i>k</i> ≥ 3, we show

$`\displaystyle r_k(N)\le C_kN\exp\bigl(-c_k(\log N)^{\varepsilon_k}\bigr)`$

with $`C_k,c_k,\varepsilon_k\gt 0`$, where $`r_k(N)`$ is the largest size of a subset of $`\{1,\ldots,N\}`$ with no nonconstant <i>k</i>-term arithmetic progression.
