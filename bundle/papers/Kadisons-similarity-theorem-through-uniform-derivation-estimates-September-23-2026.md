## Abstract

We prove that every bounded complex-linear unital algebra homomorphism from a unital complex <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebra into the bounded operators on an arbitrary Hilbert space is similar to a $`*`$-homomorphism. This resolves Kadison's similarity conjecture positively. We also obtain one universal hyperreflexivity constant for all unital von Neumann algebras on arbitrary complex Hilbert spaces.
