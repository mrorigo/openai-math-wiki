## Abstract

For every closed connected smooth Riemannian manifold of dimension at least three with strictly negative sectional curvature, we prove that normalized Liouville measure has maximal entropy for the unit-speed geodesic flow if and only if the metric is locally symmetric. This resolves Katok's entropy rigidity conjecture positively in these dimensions, including all rank-one symmetric types at arbitrary scale.
