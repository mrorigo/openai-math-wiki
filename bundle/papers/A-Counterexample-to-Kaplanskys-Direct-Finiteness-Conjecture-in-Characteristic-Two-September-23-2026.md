## Abstract

We disprove Kaplansky's direct-finiteness conjecture by constructing a finite field <i>K</i> of characteristic two, a finitely presented group <i>G</i>, and finite sums $`a,b\in K[G]`$ with $`ab=1`$ but $`ba\ne1`$. The group <i>G</i> is nonsofic. The same elements define a cellular automaton on <i>K</i><sup><i>G</i></sup> that is injective but not surjective, disproving Gottschalk's surjunctivity conjecture.
