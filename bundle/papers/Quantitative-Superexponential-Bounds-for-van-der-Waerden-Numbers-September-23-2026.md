## Abstract

We prove that there are absolute constants <i>c</i> &gt; 0 and <i>K</i><sub>0</sub> such that $`W_r(k)\gt k^{ck\lfloor\log_2 r\rfloor}`$ for every $`k\ge K_0`$ and <i>r</i> ≥ 2. Consequently $`W_r(k)^{1/k}\to\infty`$ for each fixed <i>r</i> ≥ 2, giving a quantitative positive resolution of Erdős's superexponential-growth question, including the two-color case.
