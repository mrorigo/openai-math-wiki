## Abstract

We prove the rational-boundary log abundance conjecture in every dimension over algebraically closed fields of characteristic zero: every nef ℚ-Cartier log canonical divisor on a projective log canonical pair with effective rational boundary is semiample. Over ℂ, the proof also establishes canonical nonvanishing for smooth projective varieties in every dimension.
