## Abstract

We resolve the contact-Fano homogeneity conjecture and the Riemannian LeBrun–Salamon conjecture positively. Every smooth connected complex projective contact Fano manifold of complex dimension at least three, with its given contact distribution, is contact-isomorphic to the adjoint variety of a simple complex Lie algebra. Consequently, every closed connected smooth positive quaternionic-Kähler manifold of real dimension $`4m\geq8`$ is homothetic to a compact symmetric Wolf space.
