## Abstract

We construct a single matrix substitution that detects every nonzero size-<i>s</i> division-free noncommutative formula in <i>n</i> variables over every field of a given positive characteristic. One deterministic machine, given a promised prime <i>p</i> in binary and <i>n</i>, <i>s</i> in unary, outputs matrices over 𝔽<sub><i>p</i></sub> of dimension $`O(n^3s^6)`$ in polynomial bit time. The same tuple works with arbitrary extension-field coefficients, including in characteristic two. The construction also applies to the stated acyclic algebraic path programs.
