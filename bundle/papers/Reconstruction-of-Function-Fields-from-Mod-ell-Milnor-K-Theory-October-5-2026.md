## Abstract

We prove a mod-<i>ℓ</i> Bogomolov–Pop reconstruction theorem for function fields of transcendence degree at least two over arbitrary algebraically closed fields of characteristic different from <i>ℓ</i>. The groups $`K^{\mathrm M}_1/\ell`$ and $`K^{\mathrm M}_2/\ell`$, together with their full bilinear product, determine the perfect closure and its constant field. Every compatible isomorphism of these data is induced by a field isomorphism up to a single scalar in $`\mathbb F_\ell^\times`$, with only Frobenius ambiguity in the field isomorphism in positive characteristic.
