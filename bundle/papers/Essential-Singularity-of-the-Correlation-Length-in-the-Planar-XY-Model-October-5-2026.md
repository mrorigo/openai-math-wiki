## Abstract

We prove the Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless essential singularity for the correlation length of the nearest-neighbor cosine XY model on the square lattice. Let $`m(b)`$ be the mass obtained by taking first the free-box thermodynamic limit and then the separation limit along a coordinate axis. As the inverse temperature <i>b</i> approaches <i>b</i><sub><i>c</i></sub> from the massive side,

$`\displaystyle \sqrt{b_c-b}\log\frac{1}{m(b)} \longrightarrow A_{\mathrm{XY}},`$

where $`A_{\mathrm{XY}}`$ is a finite, strictly positive model-specific constant.
