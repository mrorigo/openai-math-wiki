## Abstract

We refute Kaplansky's quasitrace conjecture by constructing a separable unital complex <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebra that admits normalized 2-quasitraces but has no tracial state. As a consequence, we show that the minimal tensor product of two unital simple stably finite complex <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebras can be properly infinite, even when one factor is the reduced free-group algebra $`C_r^*(\mathbb F_2)`$.
