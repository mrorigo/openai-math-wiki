## Abstract

We give a negative answer to Kirchberg's norm-ultrapower embedding problem. We construct an explicit separable unital full group <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebra that admits no unital embedding into <i>B</i><sup><i>ω</i></sup>, for any nonzero unital nuclear <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebra <i>B</i> and any free ultrafilter <i>ω</i> on ℕ. In particular, it does not embed unitally into $`\mathcal O_2^\omega`$.
