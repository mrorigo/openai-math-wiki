## Abstract

We prove Toda's normalized quintic Gepner conjecture. On every smooth complex quintic threefold, we construct a numerical Bridgeland stability condition for which tensoring by the hyperplane bundle, followed by the spherical twist at the structure sheaf, increases phase by 2/5.
