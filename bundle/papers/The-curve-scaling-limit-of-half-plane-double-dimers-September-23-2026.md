## Abstract

We prove that the complete double-dimer loop ensemble for the Temperleyan square lattice in the upper half-plane converges to nested CLE<sub>4</sub>. The convergence holds along the full mesh limit and matches every macroscopic loop as an unparametrized curve. This resolves the half-plane Temperleyan form of the double-dimer CLE<sub>4</sub> scaling-limit conjecture.
