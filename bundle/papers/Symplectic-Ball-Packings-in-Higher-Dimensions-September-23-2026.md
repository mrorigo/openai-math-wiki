## Abstract

We prove that, for all integers <i>n</i> ≥ 3 and <i>k</i> ≥ 1 and all positive real capacities $`R_1,\ldots,R_k`$, the closed standard symplectic $`2n`$-balls of these capacities embed disjointly into the interior of a ball of capacity <i>R</i> &gt; 0 if and only if

$`\displaystyle \sum_{i=1}^k R_i^n\lt R^n, \qquad R_i+R_j\lt R\quad(i\ne j).`$

Capacity is <i>π</i> times the squared Euclidean radius, and each embedding is defined on a neighborhood of its closed source ball. This proves Siegel and Yao's Conjecture A.
