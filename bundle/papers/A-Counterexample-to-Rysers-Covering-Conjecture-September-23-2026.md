## Abstract

For every sufficiently large prime $`s\equiv2\pmod3`$ and every sufficiently large odd integer <i>n</i>, with the threshold depending on <i>s</i>, we construct an intersecting $`(s^n+1)`$-partite $`(s^n+1)`$-uniform hypergraph with covering number $`s^n+1`$. This disproves Ryser's covering conjecture in its intersecting case.
