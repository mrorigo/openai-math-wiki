## Abstract

For every fixed integer <i>d</i> ≥ 3, we give a deterministic algorithm that constructs a simple nonbipartite <i>d</i>-regular Ramanujan graph on every sufficiently large even number <i>n</i> of vertices. It outputs the full adjacency list in polynomially many bit operations, with an exponent that may depend on <i>d</i>. Every nonconstant adjacency eigenvalue lies strictly between $`-2\sqrt{d-1}`$ and $`2\sqrt{d-1}`$.
