## Abstract

Let $`(H,\Theta)`$ be a projective complex klt pair with effective rational boundary and nef ℚ-Cartier adjoint. On any projective log resolution, minimal semipositive metrics on the pulled-back adjoint exist and have zero Lelong numbers everywhere. On smooth projective complex varieties, we also prove an <i>H</i><sup>1</sup>-injectivity theorem for rational interior boundaries with simple-normal-crossing support when both endpoint bundles carry zero-Lelong semipositive metrics.
