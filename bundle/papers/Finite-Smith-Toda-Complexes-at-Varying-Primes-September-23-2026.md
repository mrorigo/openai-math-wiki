## Abstract

For every nonnegative integer <i>n</i>, we construct a Smith–Toda complex $`V(n)`$ at some prime <i>p</i> depending on <i>n</i>. It is a finite <i>p</i>-local spectrum whose Brown–Peterson homology is $`\mathrm{BP}_*/(p,v_1,\ldots,v_n)`$ with its canonical comodule structure and generator in degree zero. Each listed generator is killed to its first power.
