## Abstract

Let $`P^+(n)`$ denote the largest prime factor of <i>n</i>. We prove that $`\log P^+(n)/\log n`$ and $`\log P^+(n+1)/\log n`$ are asymptotically independent in ordinary natural density, with Dickman marginals. This resolves the Erdős–Pomerance joint Dickman conjecture positively and implies that the ordering $`P^+(n)\lt P^+(n+1)`$ has natural density 1/2.
