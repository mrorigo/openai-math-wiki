## Abstract

We prove arithmetic exhaustion for bifinite correspondences between arbitrarily scalar-twisted group factors of ICC groups commensurable with property-$`(T)`$ lattices over characteristic-zero local fields and twisted group factors of arbitrary countable ICC groups. Every such correspondence is a closed summand of a finite direct sum of models obtained from actual finite-index subgroup isomorphisms and finite-dimensional projective representations of the matched cocycle ratio. The proof includes reducible products, mixed real and finite places, and quaternionic and Cayley rank-one factors.
