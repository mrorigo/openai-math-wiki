[Lean formalized](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/formalization.yaml)

## Abstract

For every fixed <i>A</i> &gt; 0, a learner that retains at most $`Ad^2`$ bits between fresh exact Gaussian linear measurements needs $`\Omega_A(d\log(1/\epsilon))`$ measurements to estimate a uniformly random unit vector to angular error at most <i>ϵ</i>, for any $`0\lt \epsilon\le 1/10`$, with probability at least 2/3. The constant is absolute for $`o(d^2)`$ memory.
