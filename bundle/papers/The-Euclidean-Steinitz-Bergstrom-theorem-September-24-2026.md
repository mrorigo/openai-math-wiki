## Abstract

Every prescribed-order finite sequence of vectors in the Euclidean unit ball of ℝ<sup><i>d</i></sup> has one signing for which every signed prefix has norm at most $`C\sqrt d`$, with <i>C</i> absolute and independent of the sequence length. Consequently, every indexed zero-sum family of unit-ball vectors admits an ordering with the same bound for its unsigned partial sums. This determines the Euclidean Steinitz constant up to absolute factors, $`S_2(d)=\Theta(\sqrt d)`$, and resolves the Euclidean Steinitz–Bergström conjecture.
