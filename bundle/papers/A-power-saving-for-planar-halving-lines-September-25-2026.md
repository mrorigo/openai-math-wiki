## Abstract

There are absolute constants <i>ε</i> &gt; 0 and <i>C</i> such that every sufficiently large even <i>n</i>-point set in the plane with no three collinear has at most $`Cn^{4/3-\varepsilon}`$ unordered halving pairs. This gives a power saving over the classical $`O(n^{4/3})`$ bound for planar halving lines. The proof is nonquantitative and does not supply explicit constants.
