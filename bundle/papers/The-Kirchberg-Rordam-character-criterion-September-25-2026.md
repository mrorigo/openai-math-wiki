## Abstract

We prove the Kirchberg–Rørdam character criterion: for every free ultrafilter on ℕ, a nonzero unital separable complex <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebra absorbs the Jiang–Su algebra if and only if its norm central-sequence algebra has no characters. We also prove that the infinite minimal tensor power of every nonzero unital separable complex <i>C</i><sup>∗</sup>-algebra without characters is $`\mathcal Z`$-stable and contains a unital copy of $`\mathcal Z`$.
