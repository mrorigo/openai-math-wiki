## Abstract

We prove that a nonzero effective plane curve of degree <i>d</i> at <i>r</i> ≥ 10 very general complex points has total multiplicity strictly less than $`d\sqrt r`$. The inequality holds simultaneously for all curves, including reducible and nonreduced curves, and establishes Nagata's conjecture in its strict, nonhomogeneous form.
