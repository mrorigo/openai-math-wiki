## Abstract

We prove that the Hausdorff dimension of every finite real self-similar measure equals the minimum of one and its random-walk entropy rate divided by its Lyapunov exponent. Exact overlaps are allowed, and the contraction ratios may be unequal and negative. This resolves the entropy-rate dimension conjecture.
