## Abstract

We prove that the height function of the square-lattice six-vertex model with weights $`a=b=1`$ and $`0\lt c\le2`$, in the plane state obtained from balanced tori, converges to a multiple of the Gaussian free field. For unit height jumps and Green kernel $`-(2\pi)^{-1}\log|x-y|`$, the squared multiplier is $`1/\arcsin(c/2)`$.
