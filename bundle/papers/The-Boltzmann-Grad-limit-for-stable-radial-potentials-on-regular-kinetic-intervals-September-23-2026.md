## Abstract

We derive the nonlinear Boltzmann equation from a grand-canonical Newtonian gas with initial pair exclusion. We treat stable, finite-range radial potentials that are <i>C</i><sup>2</sup> except for an allowed repulsive singularity at the origin, and <i>C</i><sup>1</sup> initial probability densities with spatially summable Gaussian bounds on the density and its spatial gradient. All fixed-order rescaled factorial marginals converge in <i>L</i><sup>1</sup>, uniformly throughout every finite interval on which the classical kinetic solution has a uniform Gaussian bound. The result allows attractive wells and dynamically formed clusters, without restrictions on the differential scattering cross-section.
