## Abstract

We determine the sharp logarithmic exponent of the off-diagonal Ramsey number $`r(5,t)`$:

$`\displaystyle r(5,t)=\frac{t^4}{(\log t)^{3+o(1)}} \qquad (t\longrightarrow\infty).`$
