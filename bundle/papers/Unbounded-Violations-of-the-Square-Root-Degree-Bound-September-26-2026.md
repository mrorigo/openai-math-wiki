## Abstract

We disprove the Gopalan–Servedio square-root conjecture, even up to an arbitrary constant factor. For every real <i>C</i> &gt; 0, there is a nonconstant Boolean function $`f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}`$ on a finite sign cube such that

$`\displaystyle \sum_{i=1}^n \widehat f(\{i\})\gt C\sqrt{\deg(f)}.`$

Here $`\widehat f(\{i\})`$ is the linear Fourier coefficient associated with the <i>i</i>th input, and $`\deg(f)`$ is the degree of the real multilinear polynomial representing <i>f</i>.
