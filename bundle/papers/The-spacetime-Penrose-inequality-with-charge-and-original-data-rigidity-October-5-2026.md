## Abstract

Under the stated energy, decay, and trapping hypotheses, we prove the sharp charged spacetime Penrose upper-area inequality for one-ended three-dimensional initial data with source-free electric and magnetic fields. The theorem allows arbitrary second fundamental form, nonzero ADM momentum, and disconnected boundary. Writing <i>m</i> for invariant ADM mass, <i>Q</i> for total charge magnitude, and <i>r</i><sub><i>A</i></sub> for the minimum-enclosing-area radius, the bound is <i>m</i> ≥ <i>Q</i> and $`r_A\le m+\sqrt{m^2-Q^2}`$. The polynomial mass bound $`m\ge(r_A+Q^2/r_A)/2`$ is asserted only when $`r_A\gt Q`$. When <i>m</i> &gt; <i>Q</i>, equality under the stated connected, outermost, outer-area-minimizing future-horizon hypotheses identifies the original data as a smooth spacelike slice of dyonic Reissner–Nordström, including smooth attachment at the future horizon.
