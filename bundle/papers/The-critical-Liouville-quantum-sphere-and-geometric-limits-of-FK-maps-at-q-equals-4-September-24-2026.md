## Abstract

We construct the field and area law of the unit-area critical Liouville quantum sphere as a limit of ordinary subcritical quantum spheres, and equip it with its critical intrinsic metric. We then prove that spherical Fortuin–Kasteleyn planar maps at <i>q</i> = 4, embedded by their equilateral flag uniformizations, converge jointly to this sphere decorated by an independent nested conformal loop ensemble CLE<sub>4</sub>. With deterministic distance normalization, the convergence includes the area measure, the full embedded distance function, and every macroscopic interface through all positive integer edge counts.
