## Abstract

We construct a counterexample to Kaplansky's direct-finiteness conjecture in odd characteristic. For one specified odd prime <i>p</i>, we obtain a field <i>K</i> of order <i>p</i><sup>4</sup>, a finitely generated group <i>G</i> containing torsion, and finite sums $`a,b\in K[G]`$ with $`ab=1`$ but $`ba\ne1`$. The same elements define a cellular automaton on <i>K</i><sup><i>G</i></sup> that is injective but not surjective.
