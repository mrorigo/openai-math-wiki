## Abstract

We prove that a smooth strictly convex planar billiard with positive curvature is an ellipse whenever a full neighborhood of its boundary is continuously foliated by smooth closed convex caustics. No differentiability across the leaves is assumed. This resolves the near-boundary Birkhoff conjecture in the stated smooth class.
