## Abstract

We prove that a nonzero real Laplace eigenfunction with eigenvalue <i>λ</i> &gt; 0 on a fixed smooth closed connected Riemannian surface has nodal length at most $`C\sqrt\lambda`$. Together with the known lower bound, this proves Yau's conjecture in this setting.
