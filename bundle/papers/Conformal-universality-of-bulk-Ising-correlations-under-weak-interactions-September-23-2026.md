## Abstract

We prove conformal universality of mixed bulk spin and energy correlations for the square-lattice Ising model with sufficiently small, square-symmetric, finite-range even multispin perturbations of either sign. One critical-temperature branch and two field normalizations apply in every bounded simply connected <i>C</i><sup>2</sup> Jordan domain with free, plus, or minus boundary conditions, and in the periodic thermodynamic plane state. Energies are centered in their actual states.
