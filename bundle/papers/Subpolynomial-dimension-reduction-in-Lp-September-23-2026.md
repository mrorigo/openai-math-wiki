## Abstract

For every fixed $`1\lt p\lt \infty`$ and <i>D</i> &gt; 1, every <i>n</i>-point subset of a real <i>L</i><sub><i>p</i></sub> space embeds into $`\ell_p^d`$ with distortion at most <i>D</i> and subpolynomial dimension $`d=n^{o(1)}`$. The target has the same exponent <i>p</i>, and the embedding need not be linear.
