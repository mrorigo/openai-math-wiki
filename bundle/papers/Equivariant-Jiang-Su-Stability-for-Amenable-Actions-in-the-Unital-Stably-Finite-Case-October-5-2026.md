## Abstract

Every action of a countable discrete amenable group on a simple, separable, unital, infinite-dimensional, nuclear, stably finite complex C<sup>∗</sup>-algebra that is already Jiang–Su stable absorbs the trivial action on the Jiang–Su algebra up to cocycle conjugacy. No restriction is imposed on the action on the tracial-state simplex. This proves the unital, stably finite case of Szabó's Conjecture A on automatic equivariant Jiang–Su stability.
