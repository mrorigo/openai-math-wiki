## Abstract

For every fixed $`1\le q\lt 4`$, critical square-lattice random-cluster Dobrushin interfaces converge as ordered curves to chordal $`\mathop{\mathrm{SLE}}\nolimits _{\kappa(q)}`$, where $`\kappa(q)=4\pi/\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)`$, confirming the Rohde–Schramm prediction in this parameter range. This holds in every bounded Jordan domain under uniform marked boundary approximation. The complete nested plane loop collections converge to whole-plane $`\mathop{\mathrm{CLE}}\nolimits _{\kappa(q)}`$ in a spherical matching topology retaining multiplicities and traversals. At <i>q</i> = 1 we obtain Cardy's formula for square-lattice bond percolation with free boundary edges.
