## Abstract

We prove the 2-to-1 Games Conjecture with perfect completeness. For every fixed rational $`\delta\in(0,1)`$, it is NP-hard to distinguish satisfiable 2-to-1 games from games of value at most <i>δ</i>, with a fixed alphabet and an explicitly listed unweighted multiset of constraints.
