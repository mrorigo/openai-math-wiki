## Abstract

We prove the saturation conjecture for $`\mathop{\mathrm{Spin}}\nolimits (2n)`$, <i>n</i> ≥ 2. If three dominant integral weights sum to an element of the root lattice, then the existence of a nonzero tensor invariant after a positive integral dilation implies the existence of one at the original weights.
