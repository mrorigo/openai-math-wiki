## Abstract

We prove that the trilinear Hilbert transform with fixed slopes 1, 2, 3 maps $`L^3(\mathbb R)\times L^3(\mathbb R)\times L^3(\mathbb R)`$ to $`L^1(\mathbb R)`$, resolving this case of the trilinear Hilbert transform conjecture.
