## Abstract

For every fixed integer <i>r</i> ≥ 4, every <i>K</i><sub><i>r</i></sub>-free graph on <i>n</i> vertices with average degree <i>d</i> ≥ 2 has an independent set of size at least $`c_r n\log d/d`$, where $`c_r\gt 0`$ depends only on <i>r</i>. This proves the fixed-clique-size independence conjecture of Ajtai, Erdős, Komlós and Szemerédi.
