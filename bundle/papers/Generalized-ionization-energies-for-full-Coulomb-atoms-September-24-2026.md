## Abstract

We prove the energy part of the generalized ionization conjecture for full nonrelativistic Coulomb atoms with two electron spin states. The energy needed to remove <i>m</i> electrons is asymptotic to $`a_{\mathrm{TF}}m^{7/3}`$ whenever $`m\to\infty`$ and $`Z/m\to\infty`$, with the Thomas–Fermi constant in atomic units. We also obtain both conjectured iterated limits.
