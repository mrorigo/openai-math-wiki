## Abstract

Every convex body in ℝ<sup><i>n</i></sup>, <i>n</i> ≥ 2, admits a covering by translates along one full-rank lattice with density at most $`Cn\log n`$, for an absolute constant <i>C</i>. No symmetry or boundary regularity is assumed.
