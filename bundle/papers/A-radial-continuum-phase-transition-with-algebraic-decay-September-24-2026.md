## Abstract

We construct a bounded, continuous, stable radial pair potential in three dimensions with $`|\phi(r)|\le Cr^{-3-1/32}`$ for <i>r</i> ≥ 1. Throughout an open interval of positive densities, its canonical thermodynamic free energy is finite at every positive inverse temperature and has a strict downward derivative jump at one common finite positive inverse temperature.
