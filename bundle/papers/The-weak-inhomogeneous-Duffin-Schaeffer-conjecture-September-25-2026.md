## Abstract

We prove the weak inhomogeneous Duffin–Schaeffer conjecture. For every fixed <i>γ</i> ∈ ℝ and finite-valued $`\psi:\mathbb N\to[0,\infty)`$, divergence of $`\sum_{q\ge1}\phi(q)\psi(q)/q`$ implies $`\|qx-\gamma\|\lt \psi(q)`$ for infinitely many <i>q</i>, for almost every <i>x</i>. Here <i>ϕ</i> is Euler's totient and $`\|\cdot\|`$ is distance to the nearest integer. Numerators need not be reduced, and the shift satisfies no Diophantine restriction.
