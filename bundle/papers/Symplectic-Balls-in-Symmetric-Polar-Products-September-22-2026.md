## Abstract

For every integer <i>n</i> ≥ 2 and every origin-symmetric convex body $`K\subset\mathbb R^n`$, we prove that the Gromov width of $`\mathop{\mathrm{int}}\nolimits K\times\mathop{\mathrm{int}}\nolimits K^\circ`$ is 4. We construct smooth symplectic embeddings of standard balls of every capacity $`0\lt c\lt 4`$ into this polar product. Volume preservation then resolves the symmetric Mahler conjecture positively in every dimension.
