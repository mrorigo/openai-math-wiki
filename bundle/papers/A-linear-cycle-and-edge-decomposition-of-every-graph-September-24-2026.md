## Abstract

We prove that every finite simple undirected graph on <i>n</i> vertices has an edge partition into at most $`Cn`$ simple cycles and single edges, for an absolute constant <i>C</i>. This resolves the Erdős–Gallai cycle decomposition conjecture positively.
