## Abstract

We prove the compact log-smooth Kähler case of b-semiampleness. Let $`f:Y\to X`$ be a surjective holomorphic map with connected fibers between smooth compact connected Kähler manifolds, and let Δ be an effective rational divisor with simple normal crossing support and coefficients in $`[0,1]`$, with $`K_Y+\Delta\sim_{\mathbb Q}f^*L`$ for $`L\in\mathop{\mathrm{Pic}}\nolimits (X)_{\mathbb Q}`$. There is a smooth compact Kähler modification $`S\to X`$ for which the threshold-moduli line satisfies $`M_{S_1}=\nu^*M_S`$ in $`\mathop{\mathrm{Pic}}\nolimits (S_1)_{\mathbb Q}`$ for every smooth compact Kähler modification $`\nu:S_1\to S`$, and some positive multiple of <i>M</i><sub><i>S</i></sub> is represented by a holomorphic line bundle generated by global sections. Horizontal components of coefficient one are allowed; neither projectivity nor a Campana orbifold Iitaka hypothesis is assumed.
