## Abstract

For each fixed dimension <i>d</i> ≥ 3 and each fixed sufficiently small positive disorder strength, we prove that the Anderson operator on ℤ<sup><i>d</i></sup> with independent uniform site potentials almost surely has purely absolutely continuous spectrum with nonzero weight on an open energy interval. The interval may depend on <i>d</i> but is independent of the disorder strength. This settles the purely absolutely continuous energy-range question in Simon's Problem 1 for this model.
