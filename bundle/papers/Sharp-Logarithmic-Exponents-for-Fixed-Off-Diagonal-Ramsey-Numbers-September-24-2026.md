## Abstract

For every fixed integer <i>s</i> ≥ 6, we determine the sharp logarithmic exponent of the off-diagonal Ramsey number:

$`\displaystyle r(s,t)=\frac{t^{s-1}}{(\log t)^{s-2+o(1)}} \qquad (t\longrightarrow\infty).`$
