## Abstract

We disprove the Chow and rational-cohomological forms of Pixton's completeness conjecture for his original relation system. We construct a formal tautological class outside the original Pixton relation span whose image is zero in the Chow ring and in rational cohomology. The example has genus $`10^{60}`$ and $`3\binom{10^{60}}3`$ markings.
