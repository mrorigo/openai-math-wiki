## Abstract

For every integer <i>d</i> ≥ 3, we construct arbitrarily large finite <i>d</i>-dimensional simplicial complexes with uniformly bounded vertex degrees and uniform 𝔽<sub>2</sub> coboundary expansion in every degree below <i>d</i>. Together with the known graph and two-dimensional cases, this establishes the existence of bounded-degree 𝔽<sub>2</sub> coboundary expanders in every positive dimension.
