## Abstract

For every fixed $`0\lt \rho\lt 1`$, the matrix indexed by odd vertex sets <i>U</i> and perfect matchings <i>M</i> of <i>K</i><sub><i>n</i></sub>, with entries $`|M\cap\delta(U)|-1+\rho`$, has real positive semidefinite rank $`2^{\Omega(n)}`$ as even <i>n</i> tends to infinity. Here $`\delta(U)`$ is the edge cut of <i>U</i>. Consequently, every exact semidefinite lift of the perfect matching polytope has exponential size.
