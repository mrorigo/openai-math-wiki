## Abstract

We resolve the three-dimensional Ball–Evans approximation problem for $`1\le p\le2`$. Every $`W^{1,p}`$ homeomorphism between arbitrary bounded domains in ℝ<sup>3</sup> is a strong $`W^{1,p}`$ limit of smooth diffeomorphisms onto the same target.
