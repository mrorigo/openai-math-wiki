## Abstract

Assuming the stated deterministic critical-reference estimates, we prove that the relative second moment of the quenched critical spin correlation in the square-lattice Ising model with independent fair bonds $`1\pm\varepsilon`$ grows as $`(\log r)^{1/4+o(1)}`$ for each sufficiently small fixed <i>ε</i> &gt; 0. The correlation is evaluated at the physical critical temperature, with the thermodynamic limit taken before the disorder moments and the large-distance limit.
