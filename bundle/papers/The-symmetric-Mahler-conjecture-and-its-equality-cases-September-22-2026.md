## Abstract

We resolve the symmetric Mahler conjecture positively, including its equality classification. Every origin-symmetric convex body in ℝ<sup><i>n</i></sup> has volume product at least $`4^n/n!`$, with equality exactly for invertible linear images of Hanner polytopes.
