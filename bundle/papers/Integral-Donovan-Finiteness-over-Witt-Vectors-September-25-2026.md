## Abstract

We prove integral Donovan finiteness: for every prime <i>p</i> and positive integer <i>M</i>, the blocks of all finite groups with defect groups of order at most <i>M</i> have only finitely many Morita equivalence classes over $`W(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)`$. The defect groups need not be abelian, and the result includes <i>p</i> = 2. The same bounded-defect finiteness holds over each fixed complete discrete valuation ring of characteristic zero with algebraically closed residue field of characteristic <i>p</i>, including ramified rings.
