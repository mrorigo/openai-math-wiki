## Abstract

For the derived <i>p</i>-completion of the sphere, the canonical weak chromatic splitting map has no homotopy retraction at height <i>p</i> for every prime <i>p</i> ≥ 5, and at height $`p+1`$ for every prime <i>p</i> ≥ 7. The classical sphere product $`\beta_1^{(p-1)^2}`$ gives a nonzero kernel class in both ranges.
