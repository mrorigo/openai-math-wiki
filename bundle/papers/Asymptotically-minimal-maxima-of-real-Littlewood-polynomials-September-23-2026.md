## Abstract

We prove that the minimum possible maximum modulus on the unit circle of a polynomial with <i>N</i> consecutive real coefficients in $`\{-1,1\}`$ is $`(1+o(1))\sqrt N`$, as <i>N</i> tends to infinity through all integers. This disproves the real-sign analogue of Erdős's fixed relative-gap conjecture. As a consequence, the largest binary merit factor at length <i>N</i> tends to infinity through all integer lengths, disproving Turyn's conjecture.
