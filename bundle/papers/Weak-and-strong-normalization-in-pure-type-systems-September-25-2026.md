## Abstract

We prove that every weakly <i>β</i>-normalizing pure type system is strongly <i>β</i>-normalizing. Both properties quantify over all legal expressions in all valid contexts, and reduction acts inside type annotations. No functionality hypothesis is required. This resolves the <i>β</i>-Barendregt–Geuvers–Klop conjecture.
