## Abstract

We prove that seven is the critical dimension for the one-phase Bernoulli problem: every nonzero one-homogeneous global minimizer in dimensions at most six is flat, while a nonflat one-homogeneous global minimizer exists in dimension seven. It follows that the interior free boundary of a local minimizer is smooth in dimensions at most six. In dimension <i>n</i> ≥ 7, its singular set has Hausdorff dimension at most $`n-7`$, and this bound is sharp. In dimension seven, the singular set is locally finite.
