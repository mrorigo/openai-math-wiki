## Abstract

We prove the sixth-symmetric-power case of Foulkes' conjecture. For every integer <i>b</i> ≥ 6 and every finite-dimensional complex vector space <i>V</i>, there is a $`\mathop{\mathrm{GL}}\nolimits (V)`$-equivariant injection $`\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^6(\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^b V)\hookrightarrow\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^b(\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits ^6 V)`$.
