## Abstract

For every finite <i>N</i> ≥ 2, we prove that undeformed $`\mathop{\mathrm{SU}}\nolimits (N)`$ BFSS matrix quantum mechanics has exactly one normalizable zero-energy state after removing the center of mass. This establishes the threshold-bound-state conjecture.
