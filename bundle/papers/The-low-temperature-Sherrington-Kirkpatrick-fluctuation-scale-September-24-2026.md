## Abstract

For the zero-field Gaussian Sherrington–Kirkpatrick model at every fixed inverse temperature <i>β</i> &gt; 1, we prove that the standard deviation of the log partition function is $`n^{1/6+o(1)}`$. The same exponent describes its typical centered absolute fluctuations, establishing the predicted one-sixth exponent in this regime.
