## Abstract

We prove the second Kahn–Kalai conjecture. For every finite simple graph <i>H</i> with <i>h</i> ≥ 1 edges and at most <i>n</i> vertices, the threshold for $`G(n,p)`$ to contain an ordinary copy of <i>H</i> is at most $`C p_{\mathrm E}(n,H)(1+\log_2 h)`$, where <i>C</i> is universal. Here $`p_{\mathrm E}(n,H)`$ is the least density at which every subgraph of <i>H</i> has expected copy count at least one half.
