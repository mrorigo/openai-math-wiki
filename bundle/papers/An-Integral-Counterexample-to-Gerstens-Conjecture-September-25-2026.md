## Abstract

We construct a two-dimensional ramified regular local ring <i>A</i> of mixed characteristic $`(0,5)`$ for which $`K_5(A)\to K_5(\mathop{\mathrm{Frac}}\nolimits A)`$ has a nonzero kernel. This disproves Gersten's conjecture in its unrestricted integral form.
