## Abstract

We give a positive answer to the constant-error formulation of the Aaronson–Kuperberg unitary synthesis problem. For every <i>n</i>, a quantum oracle circuit generated in polynomial time from <i>n</i> alone can approximate the channel of every <i>n</i>-qubit unitary to full diamond-norm error at most 1/2, after a suitable Boolean oracle is chosen. Using the fixed gates $`H,T,T^\dagger,\mathrm{CNOT}`$, the circuit has polynomially many qubits, elementary gates, and oracle calls, and its oracle queries have polynomial length. The oracle may depend on the target unitary; the theorem does not give an efficient classical procedure for constructing it.
