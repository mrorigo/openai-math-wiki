## Abstract

We prove Curtis's conjecture: in every positive degree, the mod-two stable Hurewicz image of the sphere is spanned by the images of <i>η</i>, <i>ν</i>, <i>σ</i> and the Kervaire-invariant-one classes that exist. Consequently, Eccles's conjecture holds for every sphere <i>S</i><sup><i>n</i></sup> with <i>n</i> &gt; 0.
